### Observation and built-ins

The executed sources access only these `robo` members (AST scan of all files): `task`, `state`, `image`, `camera_meta`, `segment`, `locate`, `back_project`, `move_to`, `set_gripper`, `home`, plus LEFT/RIGHT-style arm strings and OPEN/CLOSE/KEEP constants (the scanned sources use the latter constants directly). No program reads `robo.done`, a layout index, eval seed, simulator handle or object-pose field. `state` supplies robot FK/TCP, orientation, joints, gripper opening, recorded step and truncation; camera reads support earlier recorded steps, including step 0. `set_gripper` exposes native step and limit. The instruction is available as `robo.task`.

RGB-only does **not** mean uncalibrated geometry. [src/robodojo_xpl_rgb.py:168-226](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/src/pyrualean/robodojo_xpl_rgb.py#L168-L226) synthesizes camera matrices from fixed mount calibration and robot state; client depth/matrices are unused. `locate` combines SAM3 text/point masks across head and wrist views, known table plane and silhouette geometry; single-view height can be uncertain. The prior teardown describes the host's carving, robot masking and cached prompt heights in detail. SAM3 is the only learned evaluation model, about 8 GB GPU memory. Programs also do substantial OpenCV colour/edge/template fitting that never calls SAM3.

Motion is host-side full-pose IK, line waypoints / joint swing fallback, table clearance, bounded joint increments, settle actions and reached/blocked residuals, rather than a learned action policy. Grasp width is inferred from robot gripper state. These capabilities are part of the baseline and must not be attributed solely to the 54 programs. The older teardown's important limitations still apply: host table clearance is not a general object/self/arm collision planner, and line/auto semantics differ from our executor.

### Sandbox: narrower enforcement than the README wording

[README.md:60-64](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/README.md#L60-L64) says programs do not open files, reach private attributes, read layout/seed/success, and pass a static check. - [src/sandbox.py:30-73,112-148](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/src/pyrualean/sandbox.py#L30-L73): imports are guarded by a computation-module allowlist **plus workspace modules**; `open` is confined to the workspace, not universally removed. `exec/eval/compile` are removed from policy builtins. This is an in-process convenience barrier, not an OS security boundary.
- [src/sandbox.py:179-234](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/src/pyrualean/sandbox.py#L179-L234) calls its audit flags **informational**. Private attributes, `getattr`, host handles and suspicious strings are flagged; only the smaller `BLOCKED_NAMES` set is refused by [src/arms.py:408-412](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/src/pyrualean/arms.py#L408-L412). No universal private-attribute denial follows from an audit pass.
- Re-running `audit_source` / `blocked_names` over all 54 sources returned **zero blocked names**, but **17 files had informational flags**: five `probe:getattr` files (egg, both mug variants, organize, bottles), twelve slash-string path flags (pack x2, balls, liquid x2, equation, dolls x2, bowls x2, laptop x2). Inspection identifies probes of public result fields and diagnostic strings, not forbidden observation reads. Therefore “all programs are flag-free” would be false; this is not evidence of exploitation.

## 3. Per-cell mechanisms, in gap-priority order

Each entry gives perception, action/checks, budget, 2–4 likely success mechanisms and a port mapping. Effort is an **engineering estimate in person-days**, excluding rendered validation, asset access/licensing work and simulator occupancy. S = 1–2 days, M = 3–5, L = 6–10+; these are not measured throughput. Shared nonportable dependencies: SAM3 text masks, calibrated `camera_meta`, public step history, host grasp-width feedback, host IK/line behavior and immediate termination. Our route must consume permitted belief/images and execute through its safety/executor, not gain direct simulator truth. Paired standard/random entries below cover both named cells; byte-identical code does not mean equal robustness.

### Memory

**imitate_sorting_sequence.** Perception: head-image temporal changes recover far-object removal order; nonwood Lab components and SAM3 prompt banks for toy cars, black/action cameras, phones, watches and plush dolls identify near/far instances. Hungarian assignment combines colour histograms, projected area, labels and dark-object height samples; head plus wrist step-0 views disambiguate watches/cameras. Actions: raise arms during demonstration within its allowed start tolerance, pick in observed order, use left-arm placement or reach-aware right cross-carry; phones preferentially use table handover; re-find spills rather than blindly repeating. Four attempts/object, lift before home, and two repair rounds; repairs require 160 steps initially and break below 120. Evidence: [programs/imitate_sorting_sequence.py:1-90,98-137,1443-1618](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/imitate_sorting_sequence.py#L1-L90).

Likely mechanisms: (1) temporal removal masks separate order learning from identity; (2) joint assignment uses multiple cues instead of independent nearest matches; (3) waiting doubles as camera-clearing motion; (4) asymmetric carry/handover and spill search. Mapping: our `interactive` imitation route and twin pairing / `imitate_tie_look` are closest; add confidence-aware joint association and explicit post-release re-find (L), not another unconditional wrist look. [Identity journal](../../docs/journal/2026-10-30-dojo-imit-identity.md) and [tie-look journal](../../docs/journal/2026-11-05-dojo-imit-tie-look.md) show equal-cost swaps and no measured camera gain for the candidate; the points journal records home/keep-sides gains already adopted. Their dense `back_project` height probes and termination-triggered repair are not drop-in equivalents of our strict belief API.

**match_and_pick_from_conveyor.** Head SAM3 classifies the initial reference using ten category prompt sets (e.g. mobile phone, camera, bottle, bowl); later same-category masks must compete against other categories at the same location. Ray at half-height plus two-view locates; cup uses a fitted known-radius lower arc, figurine a round base, camera a lens cue. Intercept ahead, refine at wrist, predict descent/close timing, use type-specific height/part/yaw then high lift; retries re-search. Scans every eight actions, fixed 0.004 m/action with a special fitted speed, step-accounted search windows: target search to action 480, cup-fit retry below 520, at most four attempts and stop retries above 600. Evidence: [programs/match_and_pick_from_conveyor.py:1-35,114-168,191-335,648-768,938-1010](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/match_and_pick_from_conveyor.py#L1-L35).

Likely mechanisms: (1) reference-calibrated relative confidence; (2) native-action time interception; (3) geometric part fits for unreliable silhouette centers; (4) per-category grasp plans. Mapping: our conveyor interception / wrist veto in `interactive` is closest (M–L); missing wide-object/part-sensitive intercept geometry. [Points table](../../docs/journal/2026-11-05-dojo-points-table.md) specifically identifies cone interception after the veto. SAM3 score calibration, exact speed and per-asset offsets require remeasurement; do not transfer numeric thresholds as model-independent confidence.

### Open

Likely mechanisms: (1) assignment over fixed slots; (2) transform TCP around mouth, not bottle center; (3) staged jet-aware tilt/untilt. Mapping: existing `pour_order` and ported `pour_bottle_lean` (M for sensor/budget parity), **not a new pouring capability**. Category-specific grasps (flat objects at table level), align finger line before tilting, direct cross-carry into basket, batch by arm; audit baskets and recover rim/table leftovers. Read real step limit through grip, count motion steps, smaller 22-step home reserve, 70-step repair gate. Evidence: [programs/classify_objects_by_language.py:1-100,142-288,322-400,580-599](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/classify_objects_by_language.py#L1-L100).

Likely mechanisms: (1) synonym competition and persistent category evidence; (2) orientation-preserving cross-carry; (3) visible leftover repair. Mapping: ported classify and `basket_transfer` / `basket` (M). Evidence: [programs/pick_from_conveyor_by_image.py:1-115,783-850,975-1009,1270-1505](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/pick_from_conveyor_by_image.py#L1-L115).

Likely mechanisms: (1) explicit board-vs-object association; (2) handle-frame basket geometry; (3) predictive interception; (4) release escape paths. Mapping: `basket_image` route / `basket_transfer` (L). Our [conveyor-image journal](../../docs/journal/2026-10-29-dojo-h95-conveyor-image.md) and points table say the stationary basket lift/collider-aware grasp must work first. Their **44** score shows this remains weak. **solve_equation.** No SAM3 is needed for main digit reading: rectify head and two wrist RGB views onto 1 mm grids, colour/edge blobs, embedded mesh-footprint digit templates over rotations, symbol classification, joint pad evidence and arithmetic consistency. Pick digit/disc with refined view and alternative grasp axes, hand over unreachable parts, place at fixed pads, check/fix from above and rescan alternatives. `left_budget` is a fixed 300-action accounting envelope; defer new placement below 90, bound rescans to two and use 45–100-action repair thresholds. Evidence: [programs/solve_equation.py:15-60,258-337,345-377,436-497,505-688,880-927](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/solve_equation.py#L15-L60).

Likely mechanisms: (1) metric rectification; (2) template evidence plus arithmetic constraints; (3) re-read actual placement. Mapping: `tiles`, `equation` route (M). Our [row/size/read journal](../../docs/journal/2026-10-30-dojo-row-size-read.md) and points table separate afix/read candidates from validated gains; BACKLOG flags digit identities taken from asset indices. Match glyphs in images rather than inferring them from layout identities. Embedded templates are computed from public assets, not a general OCR model, and their fixed pad positions need policy review.

### Precision

**deposit_coin.** Head gold/pink/dark masks locate coin, bank and slot; wrist geometry refines the slot and measures coin in the grip, without relying on SAM3 prompts. Seed 0 plus seed 2 doubles that. These are arithmetic capacity estimates, not a claim their runtime takes our episode duration. A candidate screen with 10 gain pairs plus 10 guard pairs is 40 jobs, **6.7–13.3 lane-hours**, before repeats; do not replace registered stronger coin/imitate protocols with that example. Codex generation would add coding-agent spend/limits to future work; no such loop or spend was authorized or run here.

The concrete difference is not “they have agents, we do not”: they optimize a task-specific program coupled to a permissive, cheap RGB geometry API; we have legality gates, belief association, motion costs, guarded routes and frozen promotion rules. Searching only G3 would select mechanics without testing the camera gap; searching only familiar seed-0 layouts risks repeated-test overfitting. A sealed scoring panel remains separate from dev feedback.
