Lessons from earlier attempts on this rig (real runs, 2026-10-14) — learn from them:

1. Every attempt UNDER-reached. Objects on the table are about twice as far from the camera as the
   jaw tips, so a pixel offset on the table is about twice as many mm as the same offset at the tips.
   Use the VIEW line's TABLE scale for objects; when unsure, aim further rather than shorter.
2. When the jaws closed and the object was beyond the tips in the image (the jaws met in front of
   it), the object was 20-40 mm further in +x than estimated. Correct by the whole error at once.
3. Creeping in 5-15 mm steps wasted 20-40 turns. Move to the estimated target in one reply; refine
   only by the error the new image shows.
4. With the gripper straight down, a ball (~50 mm) is grasped by centring it between the two jaw
   tips in the image and lowering the tips to ~10 mm above the table; the jaws stop at the ball's
   width when it is held (STATE grip stays well above 0 after GRIP close).
5. Landing the jaws on top of the ball stops the arm (STOP). Approach from directly above the
   ball's centre with the jaws open, not offset onto its side.
