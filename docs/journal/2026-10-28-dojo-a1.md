| root cause | events |
|---|---|

A1 targets C1 / C4 / G and the released-object class: re-detect the objects the runtime is unsure of, at the moments it
acts, with the twin-trained segmenter on all three cameras, and no region or config prior. Design and rules were agreed with
the reviewer (two rounds) before the core was coded.

## What it is (all behind `RuntimeConfig.a1` = off | log | apply, default off)

**Perception, `dojo/perception/redetect.py`:**
- **Detections:** seg_twin v2b on `cam_head` + both wrists. Results are cached by a content hash of the image bytes +
  model id + input size and sorted by (score desc, x0, y0).
- **Two score thresholds:**
  - `explain_min_score` 0.25: a detection may explain an object, which blocks a "missing" promotion;
  - `seed_min_score` 0.5: only these seed, support a write or add an obstacle. C measured ~7 % of wrist detections at
    0.25–0.46 flipping between JPEG and raw frames.
- **Prediction:** z-buffered silhouettes of the believed objects; the robot (FK) is the occluder.
- **Association:** one Hungarian per camera over objects × detections. The gate is the asset category plus mask
  IoU ≥ 0.1 (never a centroid distance); an exact asset key gets a bonus. Observation only: no task-config pools, label
  groups, regions or counts. The unit test makes `origin_bounds` / `yaw_range` / `_placement_prior` / `label_groups`
  raise if they are called.
- **The active set:**
  - the triggers' objects;
  - touched objects;
  - contact suspects: robot link meshes (FK on the joints since the last tick) and the jaws swept through a believed
    object's box + 1 cm;
  - "missing" promotions: predicted visible ≥ 32 × 32 px in one camera, or ≥ 16 × 16 in two, under < 40 % robot
    occlusion, and associated nowhere.
- **Hypotheses per active object:**
  - the incumbent (the believed pose);
  - the runtime's seeds;
  - a refit inside the object's own associated mask, kept only within 50 mm;
  - fits inside unexplained masks of the object's exact asset class. A same-category mask of another asset is another
    object: ttt's player pieces were moved onto opponent pieces.

  Fits are region-free: every catalogue resting shape (lying too), a full yaw grid, no origin bounds. An associated
  object, or a "touched" one at a later tick, takes no mask farther than 50 mm away. One joint assignment over all
  active objects × detection-derived hypotheses means a mask feeds at most one object.
- **Support per camera:** [IoU with the best same-category detection, px, visible fraction, detection score].
- **Footprint overlap:** the share of another believed, not-active object's hull.
- **Corridor additions** and **lift identity** (the mask between the jaws) are also computed.

**Runtime, `dojo/runtime/tick.py` (numpy only):**
- **Triggers:**
  - close_empty: grip_check "air", or a held_look drop the runtime acted on;
  - slip;
  - release;
  - lift: 3 cm, once per grasp;
  - hover: pick / place, from `_refine_hook`.
- **Seeds per trigger kind:**
  - close_empty: none; the pad volume of the close is falsified, so no hypothesis inside it is taken;
  - release / slip: under the jaws, plus "never left" only after a real lift with no rise evidence.
- **`decide()`'s write rules** (reviewer + rendered arm 9's fasten_screws lesson + M's arm-8 write audit):
  - the incumbent competes on the same evidence: margin 0.1, and a near tie never writes;
  - support bar: a touched object needs 1 camera at IoU 0.6 with ≥ 400 px, or 2 cameras at 0.5; an untouched one a
    wrist at 0.7 or 2 cameras;
  - resolution floor max(3 mm, 1.5 px), and fit IoU 0.8 below 10 mm;
  - only views showing ≥ 0.9 of the object decide (`MIN_VIS_TO_MOVE` = `place_min_vis`);
  - fixture-held objects move only on gross errors (> 45 mm);
  - small objects (mesh-based) and fixture-held ones need 2 views or 2 ticks;
  - hysteresis: 2 × margin;
  - footprint overlap ≤ 0.2;
  - unsupported active objects after an empty close are marked uncertain;
  - lift relabel: log-only;
  - added obstacles: logged and tracked, not yet put into the planner's world.
- **Provider (`belief.py`):**
  - `a1_tick` runs at `refresh()` and at pick / place hovers.
  - **Semantic versions:** bumped only on a > 5 mm / 5° change or an asset change; the kinematic carry never bumps.
    Every bump is a `belief_write` event (before7 / after7 / writer), which M's audit reads.
  - `fresh(versions, corridor)` returns stale labels plus newcomers in the corridor.
  - Fixture-held labels come from `drop_rest`.
- **Skills:** `execute_pick` records the versions of the target's 15 cm neighbourhood. After the hover it re-plans the
  descent from the world on any stale label (event `a1_replan` with its step cost). Place: the existing `retarget` reads a
  relocated reference; there is no separate fresh gate yet.
- **Service op:** `redetect`.

## Landmines found

- **`inverse_layout.coarse_fit` assumes the table plane.** A wrist mask whose ray meets the plane far away (a wrist
  looking along the table) makes its ROI side huge, and one fit took minutes. `fit_in_mask` skips views whose
  table-plane point is > 1.5 m from the camera or whose projected extent exceeds 2 × the image.
- **Identical instances** (ttt pieces, blocks) fool the nearest-same-group GT error: a write onto ANOTHER identical piece
  "improves" it. Exact-key seeds and local-only rules are the guard. The write audit should match by trajectory, not by
  nearest GT.
