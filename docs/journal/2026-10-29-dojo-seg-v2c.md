## Why the watches are not a segmenter-vocabulary problem

On the p60 by_language frames, watch 4-7 come out right 2 of 14 in all three arms (v2b, v3ep6, v2cn). For 12 of the
14, the bake predicts nothing within 77 mm in any arm. Yet v2b's own YOLO already detects a watch on these head
frames: on 100000 it fires watch/00000 at 0.97, which is the right category. So the miss happens downstream of the
segmenter, in the proposal / count / fit of thin flat watches. The 12 new rows cannot fix it. They even lowered
watch confidence on these frames (0.97 -> 0.11 on 100000), because the head views of the new classes were few.
These measurement frames were only used to diagnose, after the gates; nothing was tuned on them.
