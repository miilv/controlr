## 1. Refine turns (PR #80): the fit's centre, and no big turn from a mediocre fit

Two faults in `RuntimePolicy._refine_hook` (Precision's breadth2 plug_in_charger triage, Long-Horizon's tower chain).

**Prior orientation + the turned fit's root.** A pick / place refine that turns the object more than
`refine_max_turn_deg` (90) kept the prior quat and adopted the fit's root position. The charger's root sits 18 mm off its
bbox centre, so on a flip that hybrid put the centre 46-52 mm off (breadth2 100000-100004, 9 refine events). The
recentre re-hover then read the root shift, hovered again, and the next view flipped again (100003: steps 13 / 16 / 19,
134 / 137 / 140). The relay handed the other arm a charger 47 mm from where it was.
- Fix: `policy.keep_centre`. The prior orientation is kept with the fit's CENTRE. On the same 9 events the centre moves
  10-16 mm, which is what the fit measured.
- The recentre re-hover now reads that centre shift.
- Both paths: the general refine and place_confirm's confirming view.

**Big turns at a mediocre iou.** I took every ok pick refine with a turn > 45 deg on an object not yet touched (so
the layout record is the truth) from the proxy runs on compute3 (tower scan A/B, breadth, breadth2):

| where | n | the fit vs the truth |
|---|---|---|
| build_tower, iou 0.61-0.85 | 15 | 15 turned the pillar away from the truth: long-axis error prior 0-50 deg, fit 50-90 deg (standing read as lying). 4 were adopted (turn <= 90), e.g. scan-trigger 100001 s182 block5 at iou 0.70: a loose grasp, then a place at 90 deg relative tilt |
| other tasks, iou 0.69-0.82 | 9 | 7 turned away from the truth (mug, bread x2, alarm, tape x2: fit 120-177 deg off, prior 0-71); 2 charger fits were 63-70 deg off, like their priors |
| build_tower, iou 0.98 | 1 | the one right big turn (100021 s137, undoing s136's wrong one) |

- New rule: a pick / place refine turning more than `refine_turn_check_deg` (45) at an iou under `refine_turn_min_iou`
  (0.9) is not applied at all. The prior pose is kept, the event's `why` says so, and the stat is `turn_low_iou`.
- **Default change (journal-worthy): the two new fields change mode B's behaviour by default**, and so `submit_v1`.
  - G3 never runs the runtime policy, so G3 numbers are unaffected.
  - The proxy / rendered confirmation runs: plug_in_charger main vs #80, build_tower #80 paired with step A's main arm,
    100000-09.
- A charger flip at a high iou (0.92-0.98) is a coin toss. It was right in breadth2 100003 / 100004 (the prior was the
  flipped one) and wrong in breadth 100002 / 100004 and breadth2 100002, with the same mode on every re-hover either
  way, so neither the iou nor a second view decides. It stays as is (orientation kept, centre adopted). BACKLOG: a
  prong-side cue or the in-hand review.

Tests: `test_refine_flip_of_an_off_centre_root_keeps_the_fit_centre[pick|place]` (fails on main),
`test_refine_big_turn_at_a_mediocre_iou_is_not_applied[0.70|0.95]`.

## 3. held_look: act on a sure "not held"; bound in-hand fits by the jaws (PR #99)

**Empty jaws.** `RuntimeProvider._held_check` dropped a grasp only when a second look agreed. That second look is the
head view, which must see the object at its pick pose, and it is inconclusive whenever the belief's pick pose was wrong,
which is exactly when the jaws close on air (Tools' rendered xylophone 100002: mallet believed 88 mm off, opening 0.0,
score 0.91, grasp kept, the strikes hit nothing).

Truth from physics replays (`scripts/dojo/held_truth.py` + `replay_kits.py --physics-only`; proxy physics = the twin's).
I replayed every breadth2 / step-A proxy episode with a "not held" read and checked whether the object rose with the
flange:

| held_look evidence (confidence 0.9-1.0) | reads | empty jaws | kept by the old rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| jaw opening, score >= 0.9 | 20 | 20 | 20 |
| jaw opening, score < 0.9 | 18 | 18 | 18 |
| colour / agent | 14 | 14 | 13 |
| total | 52 | 52 | 51 |

The head check said "held" on 3 of them and was wrong all 3 times; it still vetoes a drop.

**Default change:** `RuntimeConfig.held_drop_conf` (new, 0.9). A "not held" at that confidence drops the grasp unless
the head view sees the object in the jaws. Below it, the old two-look rule stands; with no head check, the grasp is kept.

**In-hand fits.** Of the 123 ok in-hand (kind "held") refines in breadth / breadth2 / step A:
- 31 moved the object's centre more than 10 mm, 27 of them at iou < 0.85;
- among them nuts and tic-tac-toe pieces moved 40-60 mm at iou 0.6-0.94, impossible in closed jaws. They were adopted,
  and the place was planned from them ("42 mm from cell", Long-Horizon's rendered ttt 100003).

**Default change:** the fitted centre may move at most half the object's largest extent. A move past
`held_refine_max_mm` (new, 10) needs iou >= `held_refine_min_iou` (new, 0.85). Otherwise the prior in-hand pose is kept.

## 4. confirm_pose: the wrist views may confirm too

The case: Long-Horizon replayed make_kong 100001 on genuine 5.1 with breadth2's actions.
- The topple worked: the tile lay flat, 2 mm from the proposed pose, from step 135.
- `confirm_pose` judged by the head view only. There the toppling arm's gripper hangs over the tile, and the
  silhouettes of the prior and the expected pose scored 0.59 vs 0.57, indistinguishable, so the tile was rejected.
- That arm's own wrist camera was decisive: 0.075 vs 0.93.

**Default change:** `RuntimeConfig.confirm_wrists` (new, on). `confirm_pose` also accepts a wrist view (>= 40 rendered
pixels) whose silhouette IoU clearly prefers the expected pose (>= 0.6 and >= the prior's + 0.15). It costs no steps and
covers pushes too. Post-place corrections keep the head-only second look: there the wrist refine is the proposal's
source, not an independent view.

## 5. Fallen after release: a doubtful release that later diverges

On rendered hang (breadth3, main 1f7e965) every mug release read "held by a fixture" (#93), and the post-place wrist
refine was not ok on 9 of 12 releases. A mug that fell showed up only one release later, as a head divergence, and the
re-estimate never confirmed it ("divergence_unconfirmed"), so the belief kept it on its hook and nothing retried.

**Default change:** `RuntimeConfig.fallen_check` (new, on). A released object whose post-place refine was not ok and
that diverges at a later resync is marked fallen:
- it comes off its fixture in the belief (the table / movable supports only);
- it is marked touched;
- the resync raises a divergence, so the program re-plans and can retry it.

Event `fallen`, stat `fallen`. This only gives a retry a chance to start. Tools' G3 says retried mugs often lie on their
side with no pick plan; their post-release look builds on it.

## 12. vocab_explain keeps a table match that found every label

breadth3 round 2a imitate ran 2 of 5 episodes as classify_objects (100002, 100014). In both, the instruction's own set
(the table match) and classify_objects found every label, at mean confidence 0.36 / 0.47 vs 0.80 / 0.65, so the

a label, or no table match.

## 13. A closed-jaw reading cannot be overruled by the head view

The case: Memory's conveyor 100003. A GT probe showed the one close at step 182 was 71.9 mm from the target, which rose
0 mm. held_look measured the jaw opening at 0.0 (confidence 1.0), but the head confirm said "held" at 0.99, so #99's
rule kept the grasp and the skill finished holding nothing.

**Behaviour change (part of `held_drop_conf`):** a sure (>= 0.9) "not held" whose wrist-measured jaw opening is narrower
than half the held object's narrowest width (capped at 10 mm) drops the grasp whatever the head check says.

breadth3 rendered rows (r1 + r2): 81 sure opening negatives; in 13 of them the head said "held" on jaws measured fully
closed (gap 0.0 mm), and the grasp was kept:
- make_toast x5, organize_table x4, match_and_pick x2, fill_pen x1, hang x1.
