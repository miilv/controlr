# 2026-10-28: stack_bowls: "no reachable stack site" came from a reach cap measured to the bowl's centre (stream H)

Method: G3 mode gt (physics-only twin, GT world, 0 LLM). Diagnosis is on M's rows (m95/g3c stack_bowls_random_173acfa,
m95/gt stack_bowls_p1) and a CPU replay on compute3; the screen is on v1 draws of 100030-39.

## Diagnosis

Prior work:
- skills journal 2026-10-24 #8: a shared middle site always competes, and no stack plan may need a pick beyond
  `MAX_REACH` = 0.52 m (blocks 0.59 m out stalled with 40 deg tilted grasps);
- #9: the bowls' wall pinch.

The rows that end at step 3 with "no reachable stack site" (stack_bowls_random 100000, 100003, 100004; stack_bowls
100002):
- `plan_stack` measures each pick's reach as the distance from the arm's base to the object's **centre**. The bowls'
  wall pinch grasps the near rim, 55-80 mm closer (110 / 160 mm bowls).
- In every assignment some bowl is 0.53-0.63 m out by its centre, so every plan is rejected. Yet those bowls are
  pickable (`can_pick` True), and the sites are reachable top-down.
- Measured to the planned grasp's TCP, the same bowls are 0.25-0.50 m out, and `plan_stack` returns a full plan on all
  4 layouts.

## The switch (default = today)

- `RuntimeConfig.stack_reach` / `Skills.stack_reach`: "centre" (default) | "grasp".
  - "grasp": `plan_stack`'s reach per (object, arm) is `Skills.pick_reach`, the TCP distance of the best pick from home
    (cached with `can_pick`).
  - The 0.52 m cap and the comfort rule stay; they apply to where the hand actually goes.
- G3: `--skill-attr stack_reach=grasp`.
