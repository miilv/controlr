# 2026-10-28: stack_bowls: "no reachable stack site" came from a reach cap measured to the bowl's centre (stream H)

Method: G3 mode gt (physics-only twin, GT world, 0 LLM). Diagnosis is on M's rows (m95/g3c stack_bowls_random_173acfa,
m95/gt stack_bowls_p1) and a CPU replay on compute3; the screen is on v1 draws of 100030-39.

## Diagnosis

Prior work:
- skills journal 2026-10-24 #8: a shared middle site always competes, and no stack plan may need a pick beyond
  `MAX_REACH` = 0.52 m (blocks 0.59 m out stalled with 40 deg tilted grasps);
- #9: the bowls' wall pinch.

The rows that end at step 3 with "no reachable stack site" (stack_bowls_random 100000, 100003, 100004; stack_bowls
100002):
- `plan_stack` measures each pick's reach as the distance from the arm's base to the object's **centre**. The bowls'
  wall pinch grasps the near rim, 55-80 mm closer (110 / 160 mm bowls).
- In every assignment some bowl is 0.53-0.63 m out by its centre, so every plan is rejected. Yet those bowls are
  pickable (`can_pick` True), and the sites are reachable top-down.
- Measured to the planned grasp's TCP, the same bowls are 0.25-0.50 m out, and `plan_stack` returns a full plan on all
  4 layouts.

## The switch (default = today)

- `RuntimeConfig.stack_reach` / `Skills.stack_reach`: "centre" (default) | "grasp".
  - "grasp": `plan_stack`'s reach per (object, arm) is `Skills.pick_reach`, the TCP distance of the best pick from home
    (cached with `can_pick`).
  - The 0.52 m cap and the comfort rule stay; they apply to where the hand actually goes.
- G3: `--skill-attr stack_reach=grasp`.

## The nested place: the target was too deep (nest_height)

In both arms, 4-5 of 10 stack_bowls episodes ended in a nested place that missed: xy 31-45 mm off, dz +10-22 mm, i.e.
the bowl rested on the rim below.

- **The approach is accurate.** The held bowl arrives within 1-4 mm of the axis, level (3-5 deg), with 0-2 mm of TCP
  lag.
- **The descent overshoots.** Still in the descent, the held bowl meets the lower one and the arm keeps pushing to its
  target. Both bowls tilt 20-31 deg, the lower one lifts ~10 mm, and the upper ends 10-30 mm off-axis on the rim.
- **The target is too low.** It is the lower bowl + `bowl_geometry`'s `nest_dz` + 4 mm, and `nest_dz` is a heuristic
  (the vertical extent of the mesh near its floor).
- **Measured in the twin, by asset.** `scripts/skills/h95_nest_probe.py` drops one copy onto another on its axis and
  reads where it rests (13 bowl assets):

| bowl asset | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| twin rest (mm) | 22.9 | 23.2 | 29.0 | 10.3 | 21.0 | 20.0 | 20.7 | 12.6 | 27.4 | 13.2 | 13.7 | 14.0 | 11.0 |
| bins heuristic | 12.2 | 12.4 | 25.9 | 9.5 | 19.7 | 18.4 | 20.2 | 10.6 | 26.2 | 12.4 | 12.4 | 11.8 | 9.8 |
| mesh radial profile | 27.0 | 23.0 | 26.0 | 11.0 | 20.0 | 18.0 | 20.0 | 12.0 | 29.0 | 13.0 | 13.0 | 14.0 | 14.0 |

- The heuristic is 10.7-11 mm too deep on bowls 1 and 2 and 1-3 mm too deep elsewhere.
- The profile is within -3.0..+4.1 mm. With the 4 mm clearance it stays above the rest on every asset, so it is the
  fallback for assets outside the table.

The switch (default = today):
- `RuntimeConfig.nest_height` / `Skills.nest_height`: "bins" (default) | "measured".
- "measured": the twin's rest per asset (`data/bowl_nest.json`, keyed by asset, never by task), else
  `nest_profile_dz`.
- G3: `--skill-attr nest_height=measured`.
