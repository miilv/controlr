## Found

- **v2d has v2cn's losses, to the object.** v2cn had the same organize_table and arrange_largest_number rows (table
  above). It made the same garage error on organize_table 100009 (pred_idx 5, not 11). v2cn and v2d share no new
  class. So these two losses follow the incremental recipe on v2b (`expand_head` + 8 epochs), not the new assets.
  Garage negatives alone may not remove them; check a v2e arm against this recurrence first.
- **Integrity: the gate's bake sets include measurement ids.** Our rule: a gate that decides adoption uses dev /
  training ids only (100000-04, 100030-49).
  - The five sets hold 64 (set, episode) ids. 29 are dev / training ids, and 35 are measurement ids: d0_51 12,
    d0_ext 5, p60 3, series 5, w51 10.
  - The organize_table loser is on one of them (100009).
  - The v2c gates used the same sets. The v2d training data was clean: 100030-49 only.
  - The arrange loss is on dev ids, so a dev-only cut would still have flagged it.
  - Before the next segmenter gate, re-cut the bake sets to dev / training ids only, and record the cut in that
    gate's journal.
