## 3. Joint 5: the sim's stop is pi/2, our IK allowed 1.9

- **The asset declares nothing at pi/2.** The URDF and ARX.usd declare +-10 rad (+-573 deg) on j1-j5 and +-179.9 deg
  on j6; RoboDojo's x5.py and curobo.yml apply no clamp to the arm targets.
- **Measured, it is an asset constant.** Over all 972 saved G3 traces on compute3, 6822 of 7394 steps commanding
  |q5| > 1.59 stopped at |q5| <= 1.585: on both sides (4349 +, 2473 -) and at every wrist roll. The rest are within
  0.004 of the stop (compliance). It is likely self-contact at +-90 deg.
- No other joint shows a consistent stop (the j1 / j4 lags are transient or contact).
- `kinematics.JOINT_LIMITS` allowed |q5| <= 1.9 ("the real limit is self-contact"), so a plan past pi/2 leaves the
  TCP centimetres from where the planner believes it is.

## 4. The switches (default = today)

- `RuntimeConfig.joint_limits` / `Skills.joint_limits`: "wide" (default) | "measured", which keeps joint 5 within
  +-1.56 (`kinematics.JOINT_LIMIT_SETS`).
  - The setter changes the shared `X5Kin.limits` (IK clips to it) and drops the IK cache.
- `RuntimeConfig.fill_skip` / `Skills.fill_skip`: "exact" (default) | "family". A grasp that did not lift excludes
  every grasp on the same faces, approach and tilt (`library.grasp_family`), not only that offset.
- G3: `--skill-attr joint_limits=measured --skill-attr fill_skip=family`.
