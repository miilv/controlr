## The four mechanisms, as built here

1. **Order separate from identity** (`imitate_order`). Our departure read was already a persistent-pixel-change read
   with our robot masked and the actor-free background; what was missing: a single read is final (a shadow or the
   demonstrator's arm can date a departure early), and a departure never read is lost. `persist` needs 3 reads in a row,
   dated by the first; `elim` puts the one unseen object last. Offline: order right 18 → 22 / 42 by elimination;
   persistence can only be measured on camera.
2. **Joint multi-cue assignment** (`imitate_pairing=cues`, `dojo.perception.twin_match`). Colour histograms and
   plane-corrected size of each object's own pixels at frame 0, one Hungarian assignment, swap margins. The believed
   category was a cue too, and removing it helped (the frame-0 categories are what is wrong). Twin pairs right
   64 → 90 % on the dev frames, 71 → 88 % on held-out frames; 95 % of what localisation allows. Height was dropped: our
   only height is the believed asset's (circular). D-C17: every pairing comes from the pixels.
3. **Waiting and reach-aware carries** (`imitate_carry=reach`; the wait itself is the existing `imitate_wait=home`).
   Far-side objects go by the arm on their side (across itself, phones by a table handover), centred grasps only at
   long reach. A demo-time "clear the camera" motion was not built: our arms are masked out of the departure read and
   the frame-0 estimate is taken before the demo; nothing measured says our home pose hides anything that matters.
4. **Re-find and repair rounds** (`imitate_retry=refind`). Up to 4 puts per twin while ≥ 120 steps remain (today: 3,
   then the program ends even with 865 steps left: 100024), hands up before going home, and a belief that moved off the
   twin's start spot (a slip read as carried: 100009) proposed back through `confirm_pose`, which perception adopts only
   if the head view prefers it. Retries are decided from our own perception, never from the episode still running.
