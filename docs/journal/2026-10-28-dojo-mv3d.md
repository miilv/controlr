## What was built

- **`dojo/perception/mv3d.py`.**
  - Model wrappers: VGGT-1B (`VGGTRunner`, optional bf16 trunk); MASt3R (`Mast3rRunner`: pairwise depth + reciprocal
    matches); MapAnything (`MapAnythingRunner`: our K and poses as inputs).
  - The geometry that puts a learned depth into OUR cameras: `align_sim3` (diagnostic: how far the model's cameras are
    from ours); `depth_scale_table` / `depth_correct_table` (the table plane fixes the scale and the low-frequency
    warp); `unproject`, `splat` / `height_in` (z-buffer into any camera), `fuse_topdown`, `triangulate` (matches with

  - The runtime pieces: a GPU server / client (`serve`, `DOJO_MV3D_ADDR`, like `sam_masks`) and
    `learned_head_height`.
  - `propose_unexplained`: raised regions no object explains -> missing labels / height suspects fitted by B1's
    render-and-compare.
- **`stereo.check_heights`.** The comparison half of `check_record` takes any height map; `check_record` = the sweep +
  `check_heights` (same results, tests unchanged).
- **Flags, default off (`EstimatorConfig`).**
  - `height_source="mv3d"` feeds the height check from the server; without a server it falls back to the sweep.
  - `mv3d_propose` / `mv3d_propose_loss`.
  - `scan.py`'s scan-read check goes through the same `stereo_check`, so it follows `height_source` too.
- **Dev tools.**
  - `mv3d_eval`: `infer` (GPU) / `eval` / `summary` / `check` / `check-summary` / `propose` (CPU), all scored against
    our GT.
  - `scripts/perception/mv/fpose_eval.py`: FoundationPose's networks on our renderer, no CUDA builds.

## Findings worth keeping

- **Never use a learned model's cameras or its global scale here.** VGGT's cameras are 46 mm / 3.7 deg off ours and its
  scale leaves the head 29 mm off; MapAnything, given our metric poses, still returns a 9 % scale. Put each view's
  depth through our exact K / FK pose, and fit it to the known table plane per view (a smooth field, not one scale).
  - Table: 1 mm in every view.
  - Objects: 3.5 / 12.3 mm (median / p90 of the top) with all frame-0 views fused, against the sweep's 4.4 / 79 mm at
    35 % coverage.
- **The head alone flattens objects** by about 10 %. The wrist views fix it in the fused map (bias -1 mm). With the
  looks (D0 frames 1-3) it is 3.0 / 10.2 mm.
- **bf16 trunk** (aggregator bf16, heads fp32 on fp32 tokens): same accuracy, VRAM 6.9 -> 3.5 GB, 0.2 s per scene.
- **As a check of B1's records it beats the sweep at home.** At 15 mm: precision 61 % vs 22 %, recall 74 % vs 50 %,
  false flags 4.5 % vs 17 %. Hybrids add nothing. It cannot resolve the thin coin stand; the scan-pose sweep keeps that.
- **The unexplained-region proposer engages in only ~20 of 217 scenes** (+2 within, 0 broken). Most B1 gross objects
  are swaps onto another object's blob. Its placements are right in position but often wrong in yaw.
- **FoundationPose runs without nvdiffrast / pytorch3d builds:** its two networks + its crop code + our pyrender crop
  renderer, weights from HF `gpue/foundationpose-weights`. As a blanket refiner on fused VGGT depth it fixes 23 gross
  and breaks 21 right ones: net negative.
- **MASt3R's metric depth is about 2x off** on these scenes. Its matches triangulated with our exact cameras are
  accurate but sparse. MapAnything with our calibration as input: 7.4 / 19.3 mm, worse than VGGT.

## Landmines

- **`uv pip install <pkg>` into a venv that borrows torch through a `.pth` pulls a whole new torch (cu13, 5 GB)** and
  shadows the borrowed one. Use `--no-deps` for anything that depends on torch (kornia, roma, timm), and clean the uv
  cache of what it fetched.
- **eval_g1's `objects.jsonl` field `gt_idx` is the GT asset's `category_idx`, not an index into `gt["objects"]`.** Join
  by GT position.
- **`pkill -f PATTERN` over ssh kills the ssh command itself** when PATTERN appears in it. Use `pkill -f "[m]v3d_eval ..."`.

## Later the same night: E3 (research R3), b1_doubt, fm_refine, grasp time

Reports: [../experiments/2026-10-28-e3-featmetric.md](../experiments/2026-10-28-e3-featmetric.md). PRs #212 and #214.

- **The multi-view DINOv2 feature metric** (`featmetric`) renders an object at a pose, composites it over the observed
  crops (head + wrists) and compares patch features.
  - With the GT pose among the candidates it prefers GT over B1's pose for 39 / 40 of B1's gross objects. So
    generating candidates is the hard part, not scoring them.
- **`b1_doubt`**: B1's own pose scored alone gives AUC 0.91 as a detector of B1's wrong objects.
  - Measure the variant you ship. The first numbers came from the pose scored together with its yaw turns (AUC 0.85)
    and undersold it.
  - d > 0.6 or |dh| > 15 mm flags 67 / 95 at 7.7 % false flags.
- **`fm_refine`**: Nelder-Mead on the feature distance for objects with d > 0.45 gives gross -9, within +9, and
  5 ok -> overshoot.
  - Adding B1's silhouette loss to the objective (`fm_refine_w_sil` 1.0, the loop on the client, the distance from the
    server with the views cached by content key) gives gross -7, within +8, 0 broken. In the end-to-end smoke it held
    the organize alarm at overshoot where the feature-only stage reached ok.
- **Candidate fusion** from B1's foreground / VGGT raised blobs is small (+4 within, 0 broken). Yaw-turn candidates
  break right objects. The detector (stream S) is the candidate source that should matter.
- **Grasp time** (C's sequences, 138 pre-grasp frames): the feature distance does NOT get sharper at the close. Shifted
  or turned poses rank above the right one in 0.90-0.96 of pre-grasp events with head + wrists, vs 0.91-0.98 at frame 0;
  the hand hides the object. Doubt belongs before the descent (frame 0, scan, a hover look).
