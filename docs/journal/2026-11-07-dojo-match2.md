Mechanisms found:

1. **Frame-0 phantom as the reference.** Frame-0 perception reads an ice cream as an ice cream plus a "camera" just
   ahead of it. The tracker drops the phantom at step 4 (never confirmed), but `match_and_pick` picks the first object
   from the frame-0 pool, so it remembers "camera" all episode, while 16 belt reads of the real first object say ice
   cream at 0.95.
2. **The wrist veto vetoes what it cannot see.** `wrist_look_absent` ignores `visible_frac`. Of 79 vetoes, 54 were on a
   track within 80 mm of a real rider (layout GT); of the looks within 60 mm, 31 of 34 with visible < 0.5 (prior under
   our fingers or out of view) were vetoed. A G3 mode-B trace rules out arm lag at the look (commanded vs measured
   joints within 0.01-0.025 rad).
3. **The belt speed is an early 3-4-read fit.** 51 of 173 logged `[belt] v` values are off by > 10 % (3.2-3.5 for a
   4.0 belt); match_and_pick measured it once and reused it.
4. **Head-view identity of the match is ~50 % precise.** Replaying match_and_pick's decision on the recorded reads:
   of 85 decisions, 40 took target1, 19 a phantom track, 21 another real rider.
5. **Track identity flips** 4.5 times per episode (89 / 110 episodes have one). Intercept error at the wrist look:
   |xy| p50 16.5 mm, p90 43.8 mm (56 looks with a fit).

## 3. Switches (all default off; RuntimeConfig, Skills, G3)

| switch | mechanism (teardown packet 2) | evidence so far |
|---|---|---|
| `belt_ref=tracked` | reference identity from the rider the tracker holds, keyed by its own read history | offline decision replay: reference right 103 / 110 vs 88 |
| `belt_ref=similar` | + matches scored against that history (Bhattacharyya + competing-category veto) | offline: target1 taken 38-41 / 110 vs 45; kept for the record, not proposed |
| `belt_veto=evidence` | a veto needs the prior in view or a stale head track | removes ~24 of 31 false vetoes, keeps ~7 of 11 phantom vetoes (layout-GT proxy) |
| `belt_speed=lock` | belt speed from the first long clean track, re-read before each pick | lock within 0.15 mm / step in 99 / 104 replays; speed within 0.3 mm / step at the pick 78 / 84 vs 61 / 85 |
| `belt_grasp=solid` | per-shape grasp plan: antipodal by `lift_hold`, waist, rims last; dishes unchanged | G3 below |
| `belt_high_lift=on` | +10 cm after a pick (an off-centre hold pivots) | G3: no change (G3 successes latch in the normal lift) |
| `belt_retry=window` | up to 6 match rounds while 60 steps remain | G3: no change |
| `belt_wrist_id=on` | the pick look re-identifies a tracked rider over every rider candidate; a confident other category is not picked | no data: needs the camera screen |

Plus `save_belt_look_frames` (dev) to see what the pick look sees.

## 5. Where this leaves the cell (stopping condition: the written account)

  offline failure modes, camera effect unmeasured.
- The physical chain itself is ~80-82 with perfect perception on our layouts, below the 88 bar: camera (10 / 17) and
  ice-cream (4 / 10) holds and the slip-then-home retry ("no intercept" after the rider passes) are the limit. A
  within-family retry (skip the failed grasp, not its family) and a re-plan before homing are the next physical levers.
