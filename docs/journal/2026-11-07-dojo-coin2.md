- At the close the fingertips are 3.9 mm (median) over the holder's top and the coin's centre 18.1 mm below the TCP:
  the pads hold the top ~9 mm of a 28.5 mm coin.
- On the lifts that failed the TCP rose 5-7.5 mm per step while the coin rose 5-9 mm in total: it binds in the stand.
- The stand's slot is 2.6 mm wide for a 1.94 mm coin (catalogue mesh): the coin can turn only about +-1.4 deg in it.
  The camera belief's coin plane (derived from the stand's yaw) is off by 3.9 deg (median; 75th percentile 9.9, up to
  17): pads closed at that yaw twist the coin against the slot walls. 10 of the 13 lift losses had |yaw error| >= 5 deg.
- held_look against GT: 7 of 17 held coins were read "dropped", 0 of 39 empty hands read "held".
- The strict-v2 official jaw value read 0.0 on every coin close (40 / 40 rows), whatever the yaw: it carries no width
  signal on the coin itself.

## 3. A legal yaw sensor: squeezing the stand's walls

Probe 1 (G3 snippet): squeezing the stand (32.1 x 11.1 mm, a fixed Geometry) with the pads, the wrist does not
deflect (0.01 deg): the arm is stiff. Probe 2 (strict-v2 `_official` key): the official value follows one finger's
travel, deterministic over 6 reads:

| yaw error | d = -2 mm | d = 0 | d = +2 mm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 deg | 0.0683 | 0.0347 | 0.0027 |
| +-5 deg | 0.0757-0.0766 | 0.0423-0.0429 | 0.0104-0.0108 |
| +-10 deg | 0.0854-0.0857 | 0.0512-0.0516 | 0.0191-0.0192 |

So value = A + B |yaw| + C d (d = TCP offset along its closing axis): A 0.0347, B 0.0016-0.0021 per degree (it
varies by layout and arm), C -0.0164 per mm. Three squeezes at -10 / 0 / +10 deg give the sign and the offset:
`disc_align=walls`.

## 6. Rules applied

The skill is general: a thin flat object standing in a fixed slotted holder (`coin_contact.insert_thin` with an
optional perceived slot pose, or `insert_into(disc_contact=...)`); the holder's wall width comes from the shared
catalogue mesh, the slot from the receptacle's perceived pose and catalogue frame. No task name, task config, checker
value or evaluator-termination signal is read. The contact sequence itself is implemented for discs only.
