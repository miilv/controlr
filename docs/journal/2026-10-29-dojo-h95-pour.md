## Diagnosis

Probe of 100000-02:
- **Timing is fine.** The bottle drains (residual 0.4-1.6 %) and the stream has stopped by step ~200. The edge comes at
  step 285-290, with nothing in flight.
- **One latches, two spill.** 100000 latches (97.3 % at the edge). 100001 / 100002 fail at 81.6 % / 76.8 %: 1100-1400
  of 6072 particles land outside the cup during the pour itself (tilt 115-147 deg, steps 155-200), and stay there.
- **The aim overshoots.** The bottle's mouth is held within 1-13 mm of the cup's centre (pour_bottle aims it at the
  centre: `spout_back` 0). The spill lands 45-74 mm beyond the far wall, along the pour direction: the stream leaves
  the mouth moving that way.
- **The margin is small.** 140-160 particles (2.3-2.6 %) sit outside both boxes from step 1, so a pour can afford to
  lose only ~0.5 % more.
- Fluid does simulate in physics-only G3.

## The switch (default = today)

- It sets pour_bottle's own `spout_back` parameter to 0.010 / 0.020 m (`planner.POUR_AIM`), aiming the stream that far
  back from the cup's centre against the pour direction. The vendored skill is unchanged.
- pour_order's pours (pour_by_language) take it too.
