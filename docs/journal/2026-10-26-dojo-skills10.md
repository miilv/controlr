## 2. The belief fix (`feat/dojo-skills10-drop`)

`drop_rest(o, movables, fixtures)`. The movable / table rule is unchanged. On a release, i.e. the jaws open while the
arm's command holds (the skill put the object there), the static fixtures' meshes are also consulted
(`fixture_rest`):
- `fixture_height_map`: a fixture's top surface in its own frame: the max z of dense surface samples per 1 mm cell,
  eroded by one cell, cached per asset.
- Each mesh point of the released object is looked up in it:
  - **held**: a point more than 1 mm under a fixture's surface. The object stays where it was let go (a nut on a
    bolt's thread, a handle around a hook's arm).
  - **support**: otherwise, a fixture surface under it. The object falls until its mesh meets that surface (a piece on
    a board, a nut on a bolt's top, a drop into an open box onto its floor).
  - **none**: no fixture under it, no mesh, or not a still release. The old rule applies.
- The erosion keeps a ring's 0.4 mm clearance on its peg free (the ring slides down to the plate), while a nut's
  1.6 mm thread overlap still holds.
- The held-look drop (`_held_check`) is not a release and keeps the old rule.
- `RuntimeProvider` logs each fixture drop as a `belief_drop` event.

Unit tests (synthetic meshes): a board, a bolt top and its thread, a ring on a peg, an open box's floor, no meshes, a
release that is not still. Regression risk is the coin bank's slot and the tube rack's holes: their releases now meet
the fixture's mesh, where before they fell to the table. Integration-2 reviews it and runs the proxy check
(coin, tubes, stack / tower).

## 3. G3 reproduction (`--belief-drop runtime|fixtures`, `heldsim`)

`HeldBeliefProvider(drop=...)` emulates the runtime belief at a release:
- the object is predicted where the jaws let it go (the believed in-hand transform at the commanded TCP), dropped by
  `drop_rest`: "runtime" = the old rule, "fixtures" = the new one;
- the post-place re-measure sees the truth (+ offset) only where perception's support model explains the true pose
  (`drop_rest` of the truth without fixtures moves it 4 mm or less);
- otherwise the prediction stays until the next grasp, or until a `confirm_pose` is adopted (`--confirm-pose`).

## 4. Skills side

In a PR (`feat/dojo-skills10-guard`, unit-tested, not yet run in G3), mode B only (G3 mode A never takes these
branches):
- `run_place`: a servo place that does not probe (`probe: False`: a drop into an opening, a nut onto its bolt's top),
  with no in-hand read, runs the same servo tail as G3 on the predicted in-hand transform. Before, it ran the
  contact-probing tail meant for insertions.
- `_guarded_descent`: honours `probe: False` and lets go at the contact, with no probes.

Written on `feat/dojo-skills10` (commit b32d453), not validated, not in a PR:
- `screw_on` tracks its nut in mode B (`precision._screw_seen`). When the belief has the nut on the bolt's axis but
  more than 3 mm under its predicted bottom (seated 2.6 mm down, then one pitch per turn), the prediction is used and
  offered to `confirm_pose`.
- tic-tac-toe: `place_piece` takes a piece believed under the board's top at its cell as placed (after a
  `confirm_pose`), and the occupancy counts it.
- hang_mugs: a failed hang check offers the let-go pose to `confirm_pose`.

With the belief fix these are fallbacks only.
