## Offline diagnosis: imitation's 20-point bar

**The dominant failure is an incompatibility between our generated demonstration
timing and RSI's fixed demo-wait cap.** All four losses begin manipulation before
the demonstrator has returned home. They terminate before any grasp or placement.
The sole success has the sole demonstration shorter than that cap. Thus 20 is the
observed score on these frozen inputs, but should not be interpreted as a clean
measure of the programme's manipulation ability or a reproduction of its published
95.3. This is a dev-demonstration comparability landmine, affecting more than RSI.

This diagnosis used only saved outputs and CPU-side inspection: no new simulator,
policy, rendering or external LLM run. The single-slot worker was left running with
its existing admission rules and limits. Low-priority remote reads were confined to
our result/demo/layout files and public source. No official eval-layout or paired
trajectory asset, reserved id, other stream's slice or sealed directory was read.

### Public task versus our generated demonstration

The comparison uses the installed public source at RoboDojo
`f57e1633ffed59761bc8e568cfe7530c4b33381c`; its task/eval code is unchanged from the
upstream-reported `cb2a37d` as recorded in Preparation. Relevant public sources are
the [task and trajectory loader](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo/blob/f57e1633ffed59761bc8e568cfe7530c4b33381c/task/RoboDojo/tasks/imitate_sorting_sequence.py),
[task config](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo/blob/f57e1633ffed59761bc8e568cfe7530c4b33381c/task/RoboDojo/config/imitate_sorting_sequence.yml),
[native replay and termination](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo/blob/f57e1633ffed59761bc8e568cfe7530c4b33381c/src/eval_client/eval_env.py),
and [query semantics](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo/blob/f57e1633ffed59761bc8e568cfe7530c4b33381c/env/reward_manager/reward_manager.py).
RSI's [fixed programme](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean/blob/e754cf43c822b91f4728777a78071fa4e012c371/integrations/xpolicylab/PyRUA_RSI/programs/imitate_sorting_sequence.py)
contains the 640-action cap in `watch_demo()`.

- **Timing and speed:** public replay consumes the stored joint positions/velocities;
  it does not normalize a demo's duration. Our [demo generator](../../dojo/simlab/demo_gen.py)
  creates fresh cuRobo paths, retimes with smoothstep, and uses joint increments
  0.008 free / 0.0075 carry / 0.0045 near radians per 4 ms record for this run's
  `--speed 1.5`. It adds 18-action close/settle/hold and 7-action open/hold segments.
  It accepts paths up to 900 native actions. This is our authored timing, not a
  replay of an official recorded demonstration. The source comment suggesting
  roughly 575-action official demos was not independently validated here and is
  not evidence about official imitation's duration distribution.
- **Arm path:** our path is top-down pregrasp, grasp, lift, carry, lower into
  basket1, release and clear, repeated five times, followed by one joint-space
  return home at 0.004 rad/substep. It stores 41 segments in every audited PKL.
  The public task simply flattens its recorded trajectory into the support-arm
  queue. Its loader does not specify an official-generation algorithm or require
  our particular path/occlusions. Matching PKL format and successful placement
  therefore does not establish matching demonstration appearance or pace.
- **Demo end and failure rule:** RSI uses three sufficiently still, near-initial
  frames in a fixed head-camera rest ROI, after at least 340 actions, but stops
  unconditionally at 640. All five waits reach 640; only position 0 satisfies its
  rest check instead of the cap. At the last logged 632-action check, rest-ROI
  changed-pixel counts are 517 in position 0 versus 4,831 / 3,161 / 5,548 / 3,256
  in positions 1–4; their respective near-rest thresholds are about 584 / 547 /
  616 / 578 pixels. This is a visibly unsatisfied end test, overridden by the cap.
  The public task's zero-tolerance query is
  `(left away OR right away) AND support away`, with policy thresholds 0.3 m / 30°
  and support thresholds 0.3 m / 40°. Query success immediately marks failure.
  The four early terminations as RSI first rotates/moves a policy arm are strong
  evidence of this interlock; saved logs do not name the individual reward query.
  The other task failure query concerns out-of-order placements, which these
  episodes never attempt. Physical demo stability is checked only after the
  support queue drains; that is distinct from the policy's visible home detection.

The end-phase distinction is material even when every object has been released:

| Our id | Final open/hold ends | Basket-clear ends | Support trajectory ends at home |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 100000 | 560.8 | 564.8 | 620.2 |
| 100001 | 663.9 | 667.9 | 691.4 |
| 100002 | 636.8 | 640.8 | 697.0 |
| 100003 | 611.6 | 615.6 | 668.7 |

All entries are cumulative native-action equivalents from the stored path. In
100003 the final release is well before 640 but the return-home tail continues

- **Objects/spawns:** all five layouts contain the public task's five categories
  (toy car, action camera, phone, watch, garage figure), one of each, with each
  `aim{i}` sharing the exact category/index of `t{i}`. Public near sampling is
  x [-0.3, 0.4], y [-0.2, -0.05]; far sampling x [-0.3, 0.25], y [0.1, 0.3],
  with random planar rotation. Baskets are at (-0.4, -0.15) and (0.35, 0.25).
  Our [layout generator](../../dojo/simlab/gen_layouts.py) uses these public pools
  and placement regions, with a fresh salt, its own draws/retries, a home-gripper
  keepout (enabled in all five), and subsequent presettling. Thus it is not the
  official layout distribution byte for byte. Settled poses can drift outside
  original sampling limits; object origins, especially the phone's, also differ
  from visible centroids. These differences may affect perception and manipulation,
  but do not explain termination before the first grasp. Saved validation reports
  all five aims in basket1, no unstable flag, and at most 0.9 mm target movement.
- **Cameras:** this yardstick uses the actual official client, `arx_x5`, its
  `camera_config` and dual-X5 configuration, not a twin camera renderer. Public
  head configuration is Gemini_345Lg at [0, -0.41, 1.308], Euler [30, 0, 0], with
  head and both wrist RGB captures enabled. Neither generator substitutes camera
  parameters. RSI's head-view far ROI is the same `(69,199,108,471)` in all five
  logs, and it detects the near objects. The changed support-arm path and its
  unfinished return can still change occlusions/rest-ROI pixels without any camera
  calibration change. No pixel-equivalence claim with an official sample is made.

No permitted official trajectory/layout record was identified for the optional
read-only asset comparison; the asset firewall excludes `Assets/Eval_Layout` and
its paired `Assets/Traj`. These five are already our-layout runs through the
official client. Public source establishes the replay/rules, but does **not** give
a documented 640-action maximum or establish how often official demos exceed it.
We therefore cannot claim that our paths violate the public task contract, or
that the same programme would necessarily solve these layouts after waiting longer.

### Interpretation and follow-up

The precise mismatch is **our generator's physically-valid, up-to-900-action
demonstrations versus RSI's 640-action observation window and the native
policy/support mutual-home interlock**. Our validation holds the policy still to
the end and checks basket success; it never checks this timing/visibility contract.
Four of five generated paths exceed RSI's window. The fixed wait cap is also a
brittle property of their programme, rather than a promised public task bound.
These observations explain why a 20-point dev score can coexist with the reported
95.3 without establishing a corresponding grasp/place deficit. The remaining
order/association discrepancies require a separate controlled check.

Keep the frozen inputs and published 20 intact. Mark this cell as timing-confounded
for comparisons with the published result; ports evaluated on these exact inputs
will face the same long demos. Before treating imitate dev as representative of
official evaluation, establish a demonstration contract covering replay duration,
return-home completion, path/occlusions, and observable end frames, then validate
a separately frozen variant and compare both systems on it. Do not tune the
current demos or RSI programme after outcomes, silently replace the bar, or change
the running worker. The general validation gap is recorded in [BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md).
