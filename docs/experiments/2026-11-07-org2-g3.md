## 6. What limits B (numeric account)

On 18 fresh pairs B holds 41 of 72 item credits at the end (mouse 17, alarm 13, keyboard 8, figure 3); the 31 misses:

1. **Figure (15 of 18 not placed).** By catalogue asset: 11 1/4, 8 1/4, 6 1/4, 7 0/3, 5 0/3. Assets 5 and 7 (shoulders
   or arms 143-183 mm wide) have no side grasp clear of the palm, and their top-down slices slip (5 pick drifts of
   75-191 mm). Ceiling effect: a placed figure is worth 25 points; the 15 misses bound B's mean at most ~21 points below what a placed figure would give.
2. **Keyboard coupled to the figure (10 of 18 not covering).** 9 of the 10 are episodes whose figure was not placed;
   a dropped or unmoved figure blocks every stroke sequence (path-clearance or stroke refusals: "no complete push
   sequence" 19 times). Fixing the figure removes most of these.
3. **Alarm (5 of 18).** 3 no complete plan (6-76 of 324 grasps reachable), 1 slip, 1 place miss (37 mm).

The remaining distance to 85 is therefore mostly one mechanism: a **support-preserving transport for wide-shouldered
figures**. That means either a base slide (two-fingertip strokes on the base disc, mesh-checked against appendages) or
a top-down grasp that holds; neither is in B.
