# 2026-11-07 — CLASSIFY3: where the sort-into-containers cells lose on camera, and two general levers

**Question.** Our clean D-C19 candidate scores 34 / 8 on the two sort-into-containers cells (classify_objects,
classify_objects_by_language). The ported sort skill (p2) scored 80 / 100 in G3 on CLASSIFY2's own layouts, so the
gap was read as perception. Which stage loses the instances on camera, and what general mechanism recovers them?

**Rules this work runs under** (Ilia's hard rule, 7 Nov): one general system. No task names or task-keyed switches,
no per-task prompt banks, nothing from task configs or checkers, no evaluator-termination feedback. Everything added
here is a general skill parameter (`sort_into_containers`, used by the planner's sort route from the instruction) or a
general perception capability (`dojo.perception.evidence`). All are default-off. The SAMBAKE per-task-bank union arm
that this stream had prepared was withdrawn before it ran (see §5).

**Setup.**
- Recorded rows: the 20 D-C19 candidate camera rows (`c4b_classifysg_p2on_*`, `c4b_classifylangsg_p2on_*`, compute4,
  ids 100005–09 / 100020–24, preset submit_v3, ported p2, numbered labels), read-only copies.
- Physics-only replays of their recorded actions on compute3 (`scripts/perception/classify3/replay_job.sh`:
  `m95_replay.py --full --every 10`, light locks, 8G / 600 %).
- G3 (true state) on the same 20 settled layouts.
- Offline tools, all dev-only (GT read for scoring only): `scripts/perception/classify3/{stage_audit,
  release_rest_offline,candidates_offline,frame0_e2e}.py`.

Data: [stage_audit.json](data/classify3/stage_audit.json) (per-row stages), [release_tests.json](data/classify3/release_tests.json)
(every release, every rule; produced with the prototype rest rule present), [candidates.json](data/classify3/candidates.json).

**Budget and spend.** 0 LLM calls, 0 tokens, 0 camera episodes. compute3 physics:
- 20 replays;
- 4 G3 arms × 10 layouts, plus 2 aborted mode-B arms (§4);
- one offline B1 frame-0 re-estimate (v2b server ~0.3 GB, CPU silhouettes, 345 s).

`who` / GPU were checked before each start; at most 2 of our jobs ran at once, on light locks. Every process was
stopped by verified PID, and PID 3613 was never touched.

## 2. Stage audit of the recorded camera rows

All 20 replays reproduce the recorded scores exactly (20/20). First failing stage per GT instance:

| stage | classify (72 inst.) | language (75 inst.) |
|---|---|---|
| in its target basket at the end | 50 | 46 |
| not found at frame 0 (≤ 50 mm) | 4 | **18** |
| wrong category at frame 0 | 4 | 4 |
| no target (category the plan never assigned) | 2 | 0 |
| never lifted | 3 | 4 |
| lifted, not in its target | 9 | 3 |

- **The eval's `perception_gross` label is an artefact for numbered series.** The eval's `perception` block matches
  one estimate per config label. With six `rigid0_*` beliefs it reports five GT instances "not found" even where all
  six are within 5 mm (classify 100005). Do not read the category field of these rows as a perception verdict.
- **Language count.** Every 9-object language row has 6 beliefs (5/5 rows), and all five score 0. The series count
  comes from v2b masks of the group's exact classes: seg count 0–2 on every frame, so the range minimum of 6.
- **Identity is frozen after frame 0.** Over 132 belief instances: 0 asset changes, 0 category changes, 0 instances
  added later. No look or refine recovered a miss or corrected a category.
- **The skill's "is it in the basket" test is wrong on camera.**
  - Of the skill's own outcome records (`truth_in`), 31 of 128 read "not in" while physics had the object inside the
    right basket. 0 read "in" while physics had it outside.
  - The skill then re-did placed objects (fallbacks, high retries, second rounds, nudges). 13 objects were re-picked
    from inside their basket.
  - Every 9-object classify row ran out of steps (988–1100 of 1100).

## 3. The container test (offline, all 109 recorded releases over a basket)

For every post-place release, the next belief the skill read was tested with three containment tests and two rest rules
against the replay's physics (`release_rest_offline.py`). 96 of 109 releases were physically inside.

| belief rest rule + container test | false "not in" | false "in" |
|---|---|---|
| runtime rest (today) + root below rim + 10 mm (p2) | 23 | 0 |
| runtime rest + bbox centre | 23 | 0 |
| runtime rest + **lowest point below rim + 10 mm** | **2** | 6 |
| container-floor rest (prototype) + root | 21 | 2 |
| container-floor rest + lowest point | 3 | 10 |

- **Cause.** A believed object let go into a basket keeps its in-hand orientation: the plush toy on its side, cars
  upright. Its root sits 15–110 mm over the rim while it rests on the floor. The rest rule mostly put the object on the
  floor already; the root test is what failed.
- **Prototype rejected.** I had built a container-floor rest rule (`belief.drop_rest(container=True)`, commit 197e79b4).
  It barely helps and adds false "in" claims, so it was removed from the branch.
- **Risk check of the lowest-point test.** 5 of its 6 false "in" are objects p2 never recovered anyway (dropped
  beside the basket). One was released over the wrong basket and later reached its target: at most one recovery
  lost, against 21 placed objects no longer re-done.

**Lever 1:** `sort_into_containers(contain="bottom")`, `RuntimeConfig.sort_contain=bottom` (default `root`).

## 4. The skill in G3 (true state, same layouts)

Losses by category, p2:
- classify: toy_car 18/22, watch 9/11, garage 17/18; action camera and pen complete;
- language: car 8/13, pepper 14/18, watch 6/8; chocolate and wooden toy complete.

Cars dominate. Their failures are refused carries, not lost grasps. By what blocks the refused transits over all
records:

| cell | other arm | an object | a basket wall |
|---|---|---|---|
| classify | 21 | 12 | 10 |
| language | 9 | 8 | 5 |

**Lever 2:** `sort_into_containers(park_other=...)`, `RuntimeConfig.sort_park`. The idle arm goes home with v3's
checked `home_safe` when it is away with open jaws.
- `home`: before every one-arm carry stage (direct, drop_held, nudge).
- `blocked`: only after such a stage left the object neither placed nor held with a transit the other arm refused;
  then the stage runs once more.

G3 arms (true state, the same layouts; per-layout scores in [g3_arms.json](data/classify3/g3_arms.json)). The bottom
and bottom+home arms ran as the pre-rename `classify_into_bins_p3` (commits 83ce33a8 / 6cd1e12a): its logic is
identical to `sort_into_containers(contain="bottom"[, park_other="home"])`. The blocked arm ran from the PR head
(46ee25fe).

| arm | classify (n=10) | language (n=9) | pairs vs p2, ≥ 10-point wins / losses |
|---|---|---|---|
| p2 (baseline) | 67.5 | 53.3 | — |
| contain=bottom | 67.5 | 53.3 | identical on every layout (0 / 0 both cells) |
| contain=bottom + park_other=home | **94.0** | 40.0 | classify 4 / 0; language 2 / 4 |
| contain=bottom + park_other=blocked | FILL | FILL | FILL |

- **contain=bottom** is neutral in G3, as expected: with true state the object's pose is physics and both tests agree.
- **park_other=home** removes the idle-arm refusals and adds motion where nothing blocked. On language 100008 a
  placement after the extra homing ended outside its basket (1.0 → 0.4), and three more pairs lost. As one setting for
  both cells it is mixed; hence the reactive `blocked` variant.

**G3 mode B cannot emulate this camera loss for the ported skill.** With `--belief-drop fixtures`, p2's picks fail
outright ("held: false", the objects stay on the table) on the first layout of both cells. The same picks succeed with
true state, so heldsim's attach model does not fit the ported HarnessRobot path. Those two arms were stopped and are
not results (BACKLOG). G3 with true state reads physics, so the false "not in" problem of §2 does not exist there.
The contain lever can only be measured on the camera; in G3 it is a guard.

## 5. Frame-0 instances: the general evidence ledger

`dojo.perception.evidence` keeps the episode's per-instance evidence:
- **candidates:** head regions no belief explains (`_perception.unexplained`), object-sized (≥ 150 px), not over a
  fixture's footprint, not on a believed object; kept until a look resolves them;
- **identity votes,** kept apart from pose writes.

`RuntimeConfig.instance_evidence=log` (default off) feeds every estimate record into it and logs it; the world is never
written. Offline on the 20 recorded frame-0 records (`candidates_offline.py`):

| | classify | language |
|---|---|---|
| GT instances / missed by the B1 frame-0 record | 72 / 3 | 75 / 29 |
| missed instances a candidate covers (≤ 50 mm) | 1 | **12** |
| candidates on an already-believed instance | 3 | 1 |
| phantoms: object-sized / the merged shadow region | 8 / 10 | 4 / 8 |

- Candidates could recover about 40 % of the language frame-0 misses, at about one wasted look per row (the merged
  region excluded). The consumer is not built: confirm a candidate with a wrist look, add it to the world, re-plan.
  It is the next step of this capability, and it needs a general count change too. The language count is the
  dominant loss: 5/10 rows score 0 on it.
- **Withdrawn under the hard rule:** an offline arm with SAMBAKE's per-task SAM3 bank (`sam_task_routes` keyed by the
  vocabulary). SAMINT now builds a general union whose prompts come from the instruction plus the shared catalogue.
  `frame0_e2e.py` / `e2e_job.sh` take any general `DOJO_PERCEPTION_CFG` and are ready for it.
- Reproduction check of the offline path, one row (language 100006, v2b): 6 beliefs, 6 found, 4 category-right,
  against the recorded record's 6 / 6 / 5. The difference is CPU silhouettes vs the camera service's GL (the
  DEVELOPMENT pitfall); 345 s per frame on CPU.

## 6. Camera A/B pre-registration (written before any camera data; parked until the lead allocates)

- **Arms.**
  - A = the D-C19 candidate (submit_v3, `ported_routes`, `ported_classify=p2`, numbered labels) + `instance_evidence=log`.
  - B = A + `sort_contain=bottom` FILL(+ `sort_park=home` if G3 shows no loss).
  - Logging is on in both arms, so the ledger's camera numbers come out of the same episodes.
- **Gain cells:** classify_objects and classify_objects_by_language. 10 fresh pairs each on ids 100020–29, new
  lead-owned salt; the same settled inputs in both arms.
- **Strong guard:** stack_blocks, 5 pairs, ids 100020–24.
- **Parity:** the sort route does not fire there, so zero lever actions; checked from the logs after collection.
- **Decision rule:** EXPERIMENTS.md §Camera A/B default. Pooled score of B ≥ A + 5 on the gain cells pooled. Per-pair
  score wins ≥ 2 × losses with ≥ 2 net wins. Strong guard ≥ A − 5 and no more flip losses than wins.
- **Write audit:** the lever writes no belief. Both arms' writers must be identical in kind; any new writer in B is a
  defect.
- **Metrics alongside:**
  - false "not in" skill records (`truth_in` vs a physics replay);
  - steps to the last placement and budget stops;
  - re-picks from inside a basket;
  - ledger candidates and their hits (dev scoring).
- **Spend:** 50 camera episodes. The agentic perceiver of submit_v3 makes about 6 Sonnet calls per episode, so about
  300 `claude/` calls (~4 M prompt tokens, mostly cache reads).

## Conclusions (given n)

- On camera, the sort cells lose instances at frame 0 (language count), then lose steps to a wrong containment test.
  Physics replays put 31 of 128 false "not in" records on the skill's root test, and the lowest-point test removes 21
  of 23 such release reads at ≤ 1 at-risk recovery. That is offline evidence, not a score: the camera A/B decides.
- p2's physics ceiling on these layouts (67.5 / 53.3) is far below the RSI bar (100 / 88). Car carries refused by
  the idle arm or neighbours are the biggest skill-side loss.
- Identity never changes after frame 0. Unexplained regions cover 12 of 29 language misses; the look-and-add consumer
  and a general count are the next build.

**Found along the way → BACKLOG:** the `perception_gross` artefact for series labels; G3 mode B vs ported skills;
frozen identity / no candidate consumer; the car carry refusals.
