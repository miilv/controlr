# 2026-10-26 — held-object pose: the in-hand transform from the cameras, and where to look

Stream: perception-held (branch `feat/dojo-perception-held`). Journal:
[../journal/2026-10-26-dojo-perception-held.md](../journal/2026-10-26-dojo-perception-held.md).

**Question.** Several skills reach 90-100 % in G3 and about 0 in mode B. Their last insertion needs the held object's
pose in the gripper (the in-hand transform) to sub-millimetre accuracy, and mode B only predicts it from the belief at
grasp time. Questions:
- Can perception measure it at the pre-insert hover? Target: <= 0.5 mm at the leading end and <= 1 deg on the
  insertion axis, with an honest sigma.
- What does an observer look or a "show pose" buy, and for how many env steps?

## Method (`dojo/perception/held.py`, `show_pose.py`)

**Contract.** `held_pose(frame, arm, asset_key, prior_T_tcp_obj, cfg)`.
- Inputs:
  - the official observation: 3 RGB, measured joints, commanded gripper;
  - calibration from the public config + FK, which matches the simulator's link and camera poses within
    0.1 mm / 0.02 deg;
  - the skills' predicted `Held.T_tcp_obj` as the prior.
- Output: `T_tcp_obj` in the skills' TCP frame, and `sigma` per TCP axis, at the leading point and on the insertion
  axis, plus `ok` / `why`.
- Service op `held_pose`; `PerceptionClient.held_pose`.

**Views.**
- The holding arm's own wrist camera: the object at 6-15 cm, 0.15-0.4 mm per pixel. It is rigid with the TCP, so the
  arm's FK error cancels.
- The head camera.
- The other arm's wrist camera, whenever enough of the silhouette is visible.
- Robot pixels are unknown in every view: both arms from FK, and the holding fingers over the openings the object
  allows.

**Fit.**
- Colour: per view, a two-class Lab colour model (object vs background) learnt in the frame itself around the prior's
  silhouette, re-learnt around the fit.
- Cost: soft IoU, plus an outline-to-image-edge term and a crease term (inner mesh edges vs image edges), in ROIs
  supersampled up to 320 px.
- Search: a grid over the in-pad DoF (turn about the closing axis x slide along approach / across), a 3-DoF
  Nelder-Mead from the best three, then 6-DoF polishes. Weak Gaussian priors on the DoF the pads hold (turns about x
  / z, the slide along the closing axis).
- A view whose IoU at the first fit is below 0.6 is dropped when another view is good.

**Sigma.**
- A full quadratic model of the cost, fitted to 60 random poses around the optimum.
- cov = 2 * dF * H^-1, mapped to the outputs.
- Times a factor calibrated 2-fold by layout on our dev frames (`SIGMA_SCALE`).

**ok.** Requires all three:
- the best view's IoU >= 0.8;
- the fit within the search range;
- calibrated sigma at the leading point <= 2 mm.

**Show poses (`show_pose.py`).** The candidates are generic: built from the TCP frame, the mesh bbox and the cameras.
- Observer looks: views of the held object from beside the pads (+-y) and across them (+-z), tilted -20..45 deg
  toward the protruding side, and end-on, so the holding gripper stays out of the line of sight.
- Show poses: the object's bbox centre at a fixed point 0.42 m along the head camera's axis, the hover's TCP turned
  about world z in 45-deg steps.
- Ranking: the predicted information of the silhouettes about the in-pad DoF, with both arms as occluders.
- Every candidate gets the skills' IK, an arm-arm clearance check and a straight-move step estimate.

## Data (DEV only; never eval layouts)

- **Physics captures** (`held_data.capture_snippet`, a `g3 snippet` on one light lane):
  - The real skills run our dev layouts (`planner.solve`, GT world as in G3).
  - At every place hover with an object in the jaws (the refine hook, kind "place": where G3 re-measures before the
    insertion tail), the snippet records: the official proprio, every link and object pose, the skills' measured and
    planned in-hand transforms, and the place target.
  - The place target defines the scoring geometry: the insertion axis = world down at the target; the leading point
    = the lowest mesh points there.
- **Kinematic variants** (`held_data.make_variants`; rendered from the same stage):
  - grasp variations: a 2-6 deg turn about the closing axis and a 1-3 mm slide in the pad plane;
  - observer looks: naive (planned at capture time from the observer's side) and ranked;
  - show poses: naive "front" (+ the naive look) and ranked (+ the ranked look).
- **Render**: Isaac 6.0 proxy (`render_d0_proxy`, the stage exported by the twin). The 6.0 render was validated as
  equivalent to 5.1 for perception.
- **Genuine Isaac 5.1**: Bridge-isr's held_look frames (86 in the jaws: 28 stack blocks, 58 tower pieces, true
  poses), copied read-only from isr-lab.
- **Priors**: the GT in-hand transform moved like a mode-B grasp-time belief error:
  - a turn about the closing axis of 1-5 deg;
  - turns about x / z of N(0, 1 deg);
  - an x / z slide of 1-4 mm, N(0, 0.5 mm) along y.
  - The leading point is then 2.2-2.7 mm off (median).
  - Two priors per frame and view set.
