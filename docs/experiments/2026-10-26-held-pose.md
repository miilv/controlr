# 2026-10-26 — held-object pose: the in-hand transform from the cameras, and where to look

Stream: perception-held (branch `feat/dojo-perception-held`, PR #59). Journal:
[../journal/2026-10-26-dojo-perception-held.md](../journal/2026-10-26-dojo-perception-held.md).

**Question.** Several skills reach 90-100 % in G3 and about 0 in mode B. Their last insertion needs the held object's
pose in the gripper (the in-hand transform) to sub-millimetre accuracy, and mode B only predicts it from the belief at
grasp time. Two questions:
- Can perception measure it at the pre-insert hover? Target: <= 0.5 mm at the leading end and <= 1 deg on the
  insertion axis, with an honest sigma.
- What does an observer look or a "show pose" buy, for how many env steps?

**Answer.**
- **The hover alone works for flat pieces held from above.** On the stacking rings the leading point is off
  0.12 / 0.21 mm (median / p90) across the insertion axis and the axis 0.31 / 1.23 deg, 0 steps. It does not work for
  box-like objects, rods and discs: there the target is met on only 12-46 % of fits. The holding arm's own wrist
  camera looks along the approach, so it cannot tell a turn about the closing axis from a slide along the approach.
- **An observer look fixes it.** The other arm's wrist camera, placed beside or across the pads by `show_pose`, costs
  13-17 steps, which can overlap the approach.
  - plug: 38 / 42 fits within 0.5 mm and 1 deg; 0.25 / 0.37 mm, 0.31 / 0.66 deg;
  - rings: 92 / 96;
  - tubes: 55 / 84;
  - pens: 50 / 72;
  - coin: 15 / 22.
- **A ranked show pose is best.** It costs 24-42 steps out and back, with the observer looking:
  - plug: 24 / 24 within target; 0.15 / 0.31 mm, 0.34 / 0.60 deg;
  - rings: 48 / 48;
  - tubes: 36 / 42;
  - pens: 30 / 38;
  - coin: 11 / 12.
- **The sigma is calibrated** 2-fold by layout: 94-96 % held-out coverage at 2 sigma per geometry class. Per object
  it ranges from 0.77 (coin, own camera only) to 0.99.
- No LLM calls; GT only for priors and scoring.

## Method (`dojo/perception/held.py`, `show_pose.py`)

**Contract.** `held_pose(frame, arm, asset_key, prior_T_tcp_obj, cfg)`.
- Inputs:
  - the official observation: 3 RGB, measured joints, the commanded gripper;
  - calibration from the public config + FK, which matches the simulator's link and camera poses within
    0.1 mm / 0.02 deg;
  - the prior: the skills' predicted `Held.T_tcp_obj`.
- `cfg.T_obj_target` (the place target) gives the scoring geometry from the mesh: the insertion axis is world down at
  the target, the leading point the lowest mesh points there.
- Output:
  - `T_tcp_obj` in the skills' TCP frame;
  - `sigma` per TCP axis, at the leading point (`lead_lat_mm` across / `lead_along_mm` along the insertion axis) and on
    the axis (`axis_deg`);
  - `ok` / `why`.
- Service op `held_pose`; `PerceptionClient.held_pose`. Runtime about 3-8 s on CPU (the simulator is paused).

**Views.**
- The holding arm's own wrist camera: the object at 6-15 cm, 0.15-0.4 mm per pixel. It is rigid with the TCP, so the
  arm's FK error cancels.
- The head camera, and the other arm's wrist camera, whenever enough of the silhouette is visible.
- Robot pixels are unknown in every view: both arms from FK, and the holding fingers over the openings the object
  allows. The pad width comes from area-weighted surface samples.

**Fit.**
- Colour: per view, a two-class Lab colour model (object vs background), learnt in the frame itself around the
  prior's silhouette and re-learnt around the fit. The soft IoU is scored only near the expected outline.
- Terms: the soft IoU, an outline-to-image-edge term, and a crease term (inner mesh edges vs image edges), in ROIs
  supersampled up to 320 px.
- Search:
  - a grid over the in-pad DoF (turn about the closing axis x slide along approach / across);
  - a 3-DoF Nelder-Mead from the best three;
  - 6-DoF polishes;
  - weak Gaussian priors on the DoF the pads hold (turns about x / z, the slide along the closing axis).
- A view whose IoU at the first fit is below 0.6 is dropped when another view is good.

**Sigma.**
- A full quadratic model of the cost, fitted to 70 random poses around the optimum, without the turn about a
  continuous symmetry axis (tubes, pens, coins: `held.spin_axis`).
- cov = 2 dF H^-1, mapped to the outputs, times a factor per geometry class ("rigid" / "spin") calibrated below.

**ok.** Requires all three:
- the best view's IoU >= 0.7;
- the fit within the search range (10 mm / 15 deg);
- calibrated 1-sigma <= 1.0 mm across the insertion axis and <= 2 deg on the axis.

Callers should still compare 2 sigma with their own tolerance.

**Where to look (`show_pose.py`, op `show_pose`).** The candidates are generic: TCP frame, mesh bbox and cameras, never
a task name.
- Observer looks: the other wrist camera at 14-24 cm.
  - Directions: beside the pads (+-y) or across them (+-z), tilted -20..45 deg toward the protruding end, or end-on,
    so the holding gripper stays out of the line of sight.
  - Ranked by the predicted information of the silhouettes about the in-pad DoF, with both arms as occluders.
  - Each candidate passes the skills' IK, an arm-arm clearance >= 6 cm, and visibility >= 0.3.
- Show poses: the object's bbox centre at a fixed point 0.42 m along the head camera's axis, the hover's TCP turned
  about world z in 45-deg steps, ranked the same way, then the best observer look there.
- Steps: the skills' `segment_targets` at CARRY (holding arm, out + back) / FAST (observer, in parallel).

## Data (DEV only; never eval layouts)

**Physics captures** (`held_data.capture_snippet`, a `g3 snippet` on one light lane):
- The real skills run our dev layouts (`planner.solve`, the GT world as in G3).
- At every place hover with an object in the jaws (the refine hook, kind "place": where G3 re-measures before the
  insertion tail), the snippet records: the official proprio, every link and object pose, the skills' measured and
  planned in-hand transforms, and the place target.

| fill_pen_holder | 100000-05 | 19 | |
| insert_key | 100000-07 | 0 | the skill is parked on main (`place_oriented` ik_fail): nothing measured |

**Kinematic variants** of each hold (`held_data.make_variants`), rendered from the same stage. The fingers stay at
their physical opening; the object stays rigid with the TCP.
- grasp variations (g): a 2-6 deg turn about the closing axis and a 1-3 mm slide in the pad plane;
- observer look, naive (o): planned at capture time from the observer's own side;
- observer look, ranked (r);
- "front" show pose with the naive look (s);
- ranked show pose with the ranked look (t).

**Render:** the Isaac 6.0 proxy (`render_d0_proxy`), 720 frames. The 6.0 render was validated as equivalent to 5.1
for perception.

**Genuine Isaac 5.1:** Bridge-isr's held_look frames, copied read-only from isr-lab. 86 have the object in the jaws:
28 stack blocks and 58 tower pieces, with true poses.

**Priors:** the GT transform moved like a mode-B grasp-time belief error:
- a turn about the closing axis of 1-5 deg;
- turns about x / z of N(0, 1 deg);
- an x / z slide of 1-4 mm, N(0, 0.5 mm) along y.

The leading point is then 2.2-3.2 mm off (median). Each frame gets 2 priors per view set.

**Scoring:**
- lateral = the leading-point error across the insertion axis (what decides whether it goes in);
- along = the error along the axis (the skills' depth check covers it);
- axis = the angle of the insertion axis;
- hit = lateral <= 0.5 mm AND axis <= 1 deg, over ALL fits (not only ok ones).
- Symmetric objects are scored at the symmetric turn nearest GT, as the skills place them (`_nearest_spin`).

## Trade-off and protocol for the skills (generic: driven by the returned sigma, not by the task)

Call `provider.held_pose(arm)` with cfg `T_obj_target`. Adopt the result when `ok` and, against the insertion's own
tolerance, `2 * sigma.lead_lat_mm` and `2 * sigma.axis_deg` fit. Otherwise:
1. **Hover, 0 steps.** Enough for flat pieces held from above: rings 41 / 48 hits, lateral p90 0.21 mm. Box-like
   objects (the charger), rods (tubes, pens) and discs (the coin) hit only 10-46 % here; their sigma says so for the
   rigid ones. For discs the sigma from the own camera alone is not trustworthy (above): treat a disc's own-only
   result as not ok.
2. **Observer look, 13-17 steps.** `show_pose` → the first `hover_look` (the observer arm's IK hint, eye, look-at).
   - Start it while the holding arm approaches the pre-insert hover: in parallel it costs ~0 extra steps; the observer
     returns during the next motion.
   - It lifts every object class to 65-96 % hits: plug 38 / 42, tubes 55 / 84, pens 50 / 72, coin 15 / 22,
     rings 92 / 96.
   - The naive look from the observer's own side is much worse (plug 25 / 48, coin p90 5.3 mm): about half of those
     were blocked by the holding gripper. Use the ranked one.
3. **Show pose, 24-42 steps out + back** (`show_pose` → the first `show_yaw*` entry with its observer).
   - Best on every class: plug 24 / 24 (lateral 0.15 / 0.31 mm, axis 0.34 / 0.60 deg), tubes 36 / 42, pens 30 / 38,
     coin 11 / 12, rings 48 / 48.
   - The in-hand transform does not change on the way: G3's measurement at the lift and the GT at the hover agree
     within 0.1 mm / 0.05 deg. Return to the hover and insert with the measured transform.
   - Without the observer a show pose is no better than the hover (plug 7 / 24).
4. **Neither ok:** keep the prior and the guarded / probing tail (`Skills._guarded_tail`).

Per class, as seen here:

| class (examples) | recommended | cost | expected (lateral, axis; p90) |
|---|---|---|---|
| flat piece from above (rings, the disc piece) | hover; observer look only if the axis sigma is too large | 0 (14) | 0.21 mm, 1.2 deg (0.21 mm, 0.8 deg) |
| box-like (charger) | observer look; show pose for +-0.1 mm (blades) | 17 (24) | 0.37 mm, 0.66 deg (0.31 mm, 0.60 deg) |
| rod held from the side (tubes, pens) | observer look; show pose for tight holes | 14 (27-28) | 0.8-1.1 mm, 0.7-1.5 deg (0.6 mm, 0.3-1.0 deg) |
| thin disc by the rim (coin) | observer look, never own-only | 17 (42) | 0.61 mm, 1.3 deg (0.25 mm, 0.62 deg) |

- **plug's +-0.1 mm blades.** The best option measures 0.15 / 0.31 mm (median / p90), not 0.1 mm. As the Skills-5
  arithmetic said, a short pressed spiral of about 0.3-0.5 mm radius after adopting the measured transform is still
  needed. That is a few probes, not hundreds.
- **Rings.** 0.12 / 0.21 mm is well inside the 0.65 mm hole clearance and the checker's 1 mm pair check.

## Caveats / open

- These are 6.0 renders of physics states plus kinematic variants; the same physics (G3), not mode-B episodes.
  - The step costs are straight-move estimates from the skills' own IK.
  - The naive looks and shows were planned with the skills' collision model; the ranked ones only with IK + arm-arm
    clearance.
  - The real end-to-end test is G3 `--mode-b` with `provider.held_pose`, after integration-2 wires it.
- The prior error is our model of mode B's grasp-time error. Larger belief errors (> 4 mm / 5 deg) are outside the
  measured range (search 10 mm / 15 deg).
- insert_key: not measured (the skill is parked on main).
- `show_pose`'s predicted sigma is far below the measured error and is used for ranking only (BACKLOG).
- The own-camera ambiguity of box-like objects (the turn about the closing axis vs the slide along the approach) is
  physical for that viewpoint. A flat-shading NCC term did not fix it on 5.1 blocks (2 better, 6 worse).
