# 2026-10-25 — scan pose at episode start: B1 with an unoccluded head view

Follow-up to [perception-options](2026-10-25-perception-options.md) §4.1. Question: does a fixed park-and-look pose pair
at episode start shrink B1's largest failure class, objects hidden under the home grippers? Code:
`dojo/perception/scan.py` (pose), `dojo/perception/eval_g1.py` (`--look-primary`, scan frames in the clean
background). Data: our D0 copy (stack_blocks, push_T, build_tower; 25 layouts each). GT is read only by the scorer.

## Pose
- **Definition.** Left wrist camera eye (-0.44, -0.46, 1.06), looking at (-0.34, -0.14, table); the right arm mirrors
  it. Flange poses come from the public mount, joints from IK nearest to home, and the pair is the same in every
  episode.
- **Design search** (FK + robot masks only):
  - head-view occlusion of the object region (x ±0.47, y -0.27..0.10): 0 % at the scan pose vs 12.5 % at home;
  - each wrist camera sees its whole table-front corner zone at about 0.43 m (about 1.1 mm per pixel).
- **Reached in the 5.1 physics twin** (simlab `look_render`, cuRobo): 75/75 layouts, camera within 1.2 cm / 2.7 deg.
  The move out costs 36 env steps with simlab's Mover, and a return about the same. Rendered with the Isaac 6.0 proxy
  (`frame_S1`).

## Result (within tolerance / gross, 325 objects)

| arm | tower (200) | T (25) | pad (25) | stack blocks (75) | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| frame 0 (B1 today) | 168 / 30 | 21 / 4 | 20 / 4 | 63 / 7 | 272 / 45 |
| scan fused onto frame 0, conservative fuse | 169 / 29 | 21 / 4 | 20 / 4 | 63 / 7 | 273 / 44 |
| scan fused onto frame 0, full fuse | 171 / 25 | 22 / 3 | 16 / 8 | 60 / 11 | 269 / 47 |
| scan as the primary view alone, old background | 152 / 43 | 21 / 4 | 12 / 13 | 62 / 12 | 247 / 72 |
| scan primary + frame 0, old background | 171 / 27 | 21 / 4 | 18 / 7 | 62 / 10 | 272 / 48 |
| scan primary alone, scan-aware background | 169 / 28 | 25 / 0 | 14 / 11 | 66 / 6 | 274 / 45 |
| scan primary + frame 0, scan-aware background | 187 / 10 | 25 / 0 | 18 / 7 | 67 / 5 | 297 / 22 |
| **same + frame-0 fill for objects the scan view misses** | **187 / 10** | **25 / 0** | **22 / 3** | **70 / 2** | **304 / 15** |

- **The background was the blocker.** B1's robot-free head background is a masked median over frames 0-3. In those
  frames the zone under the home grippers is always covered, so B1 marks it "unseen" and reads every pixel there as
  unknown, including the objects the scan reveals.
- **The fix is to build the clean background with scan frames too.** That is offline data of ours; the production
  background must include them (`--build-bg-folds` on a D0 that has `frame_S1`).
- **The conservative fuse cannot add objects.** It only refines frame 0's objects, so it shows no gain by
  construction. Proposals come from the primary head view, which is why the scan must be the primary view.
- **Pads are missed more often from the scan view** (7 of 25): a flat grey pad is weak in the farther wrist views. The
  frame-0 fill (take frame 0's reading for an object the scan estimate does not find) recovers them.
- **Net effect:** within tolerance 83.7 % -> 93.5 %, gross errors 45 -> 15 (-67 %), for about 36 env steps out
  (+ about 36 back if the policy returns home), 0 LLM calls.
