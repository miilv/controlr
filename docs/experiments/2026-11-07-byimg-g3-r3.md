## Diagnosis and development probes

The task checker is binary: target origin >reset+80 mm and target on the
basket bottom (gap -10..40 mm, covered footprint). It supplies no graded
partial credit and latches immediately, without testing jaw opening or a
stationary endpoint. The installed public checker and native termination were
read before these experiments. Only unscored continuation probes suppress a
positive latch; production and the registered panel do not.

Round 2's first two successes have different endpoints. Exact action replay
reproduces 100012 at action 254 with the target jaw closed and 100017 at 304
with it open. Continuing the skill function plus 100 stationary actions
leaves 100012 only **14.3 mm** above reset; 100017 remains **119.7 mm** above
reset with **32.5 mm** bottom gap. Thus one of those two primary successes
survives **skill-only** completion; the other is a transient coincidence.
These earlier probes bypassed `planner.solve` and its final `finish()`; they
do not establish a stable full-G3 endpoint. These are spent
layout diagnoses, not a new success-rate estimate.

The four carry/drop endings also have distinct mechanisms:

These are actual physics records, not commanded paths. Large wrist rotation
explains only part of the loss. Even with little target translation, basket
rolling can invalidate the drop. The baseline 50 mm lowering itself rotates
the basket 9–13° and shifts the rim attachment 4–16 mm. Glue's loaded lift
shifts its rim attachment 95 mm while the basket bottom stays near 771 mm;
100017 instead raises its bottom from 806 to 890 mm.

The frozen B carry keeps the wrist fixed, uses 0.025 rad/action with easing,
checks six stationary squeeze actions, and searches nine projected-footprint
inset sites. It rejects attachment drift >20 mm before carry / >40 mm after,
and a >40 mm alignment error without opening. A lower diagonal entry lost the
target on contact; a slower joint fallback and rotation fallback did not solve
retention. Bringing the basket closer lost its attachment by 93–108 mm in
under-target pilots. A second rim pinch and synchronous 100 mm lift produced
a **released-target native success** on glue, but continuation ended tilted
14.4° with 50 mm bottom gap. Half-tilt feedback ended with 42.6 mm gap, still
outside 40 mm. This is not a stable gain.

An alternative raised the empty basket under the retained target before
opening. Five measured corrections reached an 868 mm basket bottom / 3.6°

completion+100 actions the basket bottom fell to **817 mm**, about **51 mm**
lower, and the target failed the 80 mm rise condition. The zero-offset version
stalled at 833 mm after six corrections. The actual arm tracked these commands
within 0.0004 rad in the earlier settle probe: extra waiting alone does not
make the basket follow the assumed rigid rim attachment.

### Full-controller cleanup landmine

Inspecting the panel logs exposed an additional endpoint issue: the shared
G3 `planner.solve()` calls `Skills.finish()` after either success or failure.
That opens both grippers and homes both arms, including the basket holder.
The earlier direct-skill continuations held the commanded basket pose and
therefore did not test this cleanup. Their geometric measurements remain valid
for the skill, but must not be described as stable full-controller placements.
The endpoint audit runs the full planner, its finish, and the final three G3
quiet actions before the 100-action hold. Native panel behavior is unchanged.
A held-basket route also needs an explicitly scoped final-hold policy; changing
that shared behavior after panel outcomes would invalidate this frozen test,
so it is documented for the next bundle rather than silently patched here.

The full-planner diagnostic on spent R2 layout 100017 reproduces the first
native latch at action 304 and target release at 276. After normal `finish()`
and the final three G3 quiet actions plus 100 stationary actions, the target
origin is **-32.1 mm** relative to reset, basket bottom **758.5 mm**, bottom gap
**9.7 mm**, and target/basket relative drift 50.2 mm. Current native terms are
lift=0, inside=1. Its full controller ends at 449/700 actions and does **not**
sustain the skill-only 119.7 mm elevation. This baseline diagnostic is
unscored and separate from the 16-pair official comparison. It confirms that
cleanup releases the basket; target release alone does not establish the
intended raised end state. The exact row is included in the paired-results
artifact as a separately named diagnostic, not in n.
