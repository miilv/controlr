## Summary
- **End to end** (B1 + `seg_propose`, paired, GPU GL, 201 held-out frame-0 scenes / 1013 objects):
  - v2b + mask de-dup: within tolerance 902 -> **951**, **50 fixed / 1 broken**, scenes all right 128 -> 150;
  - v1: 942 (40 / 0);
  - GT-mask ceiling: 954 (53 / 1).
  - The one break is a pen at 15.3 mm against an assumed 15 mm grasp tolerance. Adopted for the rendered arm (lead).
- **Component** (held-out frames, head view, task objects, v1 -> v2b): recall 0.91 -> 0.94, IoU 0.84 -> 0.88, asset
  class 0.77 -> 0.83. On B1-gross objects: recall 0.81 -> 0.91, class 0.66 -> 0.78.
- **make_kong glyph class** is still weak: head 0.35, wrist at hover 0.27 on 11 val views. That task needs a
  dedicated wrist-crop classifier.

## Data and training
- **Renders** (genuine 5.1, compute2, 05:49-09:25Z, on top of v1's 18 tasks):
  - the 18 other tasks we play (variant 1);
  - variants 2-3 of general_pickup and pack_random (clutter);
  - variant 2 of make_kong, stack_blocks, stack_blocks_by_language, stack_bowls, organize_table, build_tower and
    put_bottles (look-alikes, B1-gross).
  - Totals: 12,351 images (11,769 train / 582 val = the 100049 draws), 55,079 instance masks, 511 asset classes.
- **Export.** LVIS repeat-factor sampling, t = 0.02 (`export_yolo.py --rfs-t`): the train images are listed 22,259
  times.
- **v2b.** YOLO11s-seg fine-tuned from v1, 960 px, 20 epochs, batch 16. Training took 1.21 h on the 4090 at ~297 W
  box power (throttle target 380 W, never reached). Val mask mAP50 0.888, mAP50-95 0.798, over 36 tasks.
  - The first launch at 40 epochs (3.9 min / epoch, 2.6 h) was cut after one epoch.

- **v2b vs v1, paired:** +13 / -4.
  - The 4: two tower blocks at 5.0-5.9 deg against a 5 deg rotation tolerance, a block at 6 -> 17 mm, and the pen.
- **The de-dup.** The detector's NMS is per class, so one object can come out twice under two classes (stack_blocks
  100000: a beige block as block/00015 and block/00014). The duplicate counted as an "unexplained mask". A correct
  member that the detector had missed (a purple block by the left gripper) was then moved onto it: 3.8 -> 744 mm.
  - `_seg_dedup` drops masks that overlap a higher-scoring one by IoU > `seg_dup_iou` (0.7, default on inside
    `seg_propose`) and keeps their class in `alts`.
  - v1 is unchanged with it.
