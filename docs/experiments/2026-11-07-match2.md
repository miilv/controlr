## 3. Conclusions (given n)

- The camera gap to G3 (~50 vs ~80 on the same layouts) is perception decisions: the remembered reference (20 of 61
  failures), the wrist veto rejecting the only true match (14), and the match's identity (21 of 85 replayed decisions
  took another real rider). `belt_ref=tracked`, `belt_veto=evidence` and `belt_speed=lock` remove measured offline
  failure modes (reference 88 -> 103 / 110; speed 61 -> 78 within 0.3 mm / step; ~24 of 31 false vetoes). Their camera
  effect is not measured: offline replays keep the robot at home and stop at the 60th logged read.
- Even with perfect perception the chain scores ~80-82 here. Reaching 88 needs the physical stages too: camera / ice
  cream holds and the retry after a slip (the rider often passes out of reach while the arm homes: "no intercept").
- Rough projection, not a result: if the three perception switches recover the reference and veto losses at the
  conditional success rate seen today (~54 %), the cell rises to about 60-65 on these layouts. **Not on track to 88
  without a physical-chain gain as well.**
- `belt_ref=similar` (reference-calibrated similarity for matches) and `belt_grasp=solid` are measured nulls / losses.
