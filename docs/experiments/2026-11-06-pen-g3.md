# PEN: pen-holder G3 ceiling and holder stability

**Question.** Can new grasp candidates and holder/drop geometry recover pen-holder
skill score under perfect perception?

**Setup.** Physics-only G3 on compute6, `planner.solve` with GT state, no LLM.
Baseline replication: settled IDs 100000–04. Paired development validation:
ten fresh variant-2 draws 100030–39, generated and presettled privately under
`~/dojo-data/pen/simlab/layouts/fill_pen_holder`; all ten stable. No sealed or
reserved layouts used. No camera runs. Both final arms use single-thread BLAS,
MemoryMax=10G / CPUQuota=600%, one of two light locks, used VRAM <=24 GiB at
admission. Config axes default off; see the journal for command lines and code
freezes. Report score is 100 times the checker's reward.

## Failure classes and mechanisms

Original baseline, 20 pens: 10 inside at their last fill event, 4 place misses,
2 unreachable transfers, 4 final grasp slips. All four pen/00000 appearances
slip, with nine failed lift attempts; reported rise 0–3 mm for an 80 mm lift.
No original-baseline holder ends tipped. The fresh baseline tips **4/10**
holders; the combination tips **0/10**.

Fresh baseline's last fill events: inside 5, place miss (including `ok` followed
by not-inside) 13, unreachable 19, slip 3. Combination: inside 15, misses 3,
unreachable 11, slip 4, no opening 6, collision 1. These are per-pen last logged
attempts, not independent trials or final checker counts. A banked tier can
survive a later tip. Physical traces distinguish these from episode categories.

The combination recovers holder stability and more completed placements, but
leaves grasp failures and capacity failures. Centered first placements can
leave too little room for another pen. The new inset-packing screen retains
occupied-object constraints and tries a 7 mm wall margin before the center fit.

A recorded-pose IK diagnostic explains the null straight-down preference:
pen0's ten straight-down pinches have 0/10 IK solutions per arm. Keeping the
jaws horizontal and tilting the approach to 25/40 degrees reaches 10/10 on the
left. Yet a single physical probe with the permissive tilted preference still
chooses the old slipping pinch and scores 10, equal to A. Pick feasibility alone
does not establish a grasp-place pair. The stricter rolled-relay probe physically
picked pen0 (2.4 mm drift), but its relay stopped on a palm/table collision.
A 20 mm intermediate short-drop relay completed the relay and then missed the
holder drop, still scoring 10. Combining it with stay/inset/safe scored 0 on
100002. These single-layout probes are rejected; they do not replace or establish
a completed paired comparison.

## Validation, cost and camera gate

Full local suite passed (1795 passed, 187 skipped) before the additional opt-in
modes; focused tests cover the additional modes and configuration parity. Final
source validation and CI are recorded in the PR/journal. No default changed.

The completed fresh baseline, rolled and combination arms consumed respectively
1208.6 / 1127.5 / 1208.9 episode wall seconds, excluding reset/startup. LLM calls
and tokens: **0**. The data root contains episode JSONL, gzip action trajectories,
and failed-episode physics traces. Camera videos were not produced in the
physics-only light lanes.

The [camera pre-registration](2026-11-06-pen-inset-camera-prereg.md) uses the default
rule: >=10 gain pairs, >=5 pooled points, success wins >=2x losses and >=2 net
wins; corresponding guard limits if a shared route is found. G3 does not satisfy
or substitute for that rendered rule. The successful partial-score screen is
not an adoption decision. Defaults remain off and the PR is for lead review.
