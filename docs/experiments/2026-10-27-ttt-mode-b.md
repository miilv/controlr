## What blocks the game

1. **The opponent's move never reaches the belief** (fixed by #107). No grasp of ours, no release, no post-place:
   `play_board_game` counted occupancy from the belief and read "own 1 opp 0" after the first turn (4/4 at 0.1). #107:
   occupancy = the belief + every cell a watch read saw the opponent land in; a move = a new cell.
2. **A wrong in-hand refine** (BACKLOG): held_look's refine moved a held piece's belief 40-42 mm at iou 0.66-0.67 and was
   adopted (v2 100003 / 100004; breadth2's 41.7 / 42.1 mm place_miss). The belief then said "42 mm from the cell"; the
   re-pick at the believed spot closed on air during the opponent's turn, and the task's failure query (our arm away from
   origin while the opponent's arm moves) ended the episode at step 191. #107 counts a place seen in its cell by two
   watch reads as placed.
3. **The cell reads drop and flip pieces on 5.1 frames** (BACKLOG; all 8 losses of the #107 run end in "wait opponent
   (timeout)" or stages_left 1). The logged boards show a board with three pieces read empty, own pieces read as the
   opponent's for a read, and the opponent's piece seen over cells while it carries it (`.o...o..x` -> `.....o..x` ->
   `........x`). Reading the board as a burst of 6 reads after the opponent's arm has come to rest (>= 3 votes) is
   no better (paired: equal on 7 layouts, +0.25 / -0.7 / +0.25 on 3; not merged): in its one big loss the read path had
   already accepted the opponent's piece in transit over a cell before the arm rested (branch
   `feat/dojo-longh-ttt-actor` v2 suppresses that; G3 only).
4. Minor (3 of 10): the right arm's cross-reach pick of the far-left piece closes beside it twice (~40 steps).
