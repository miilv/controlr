# POUR: language pouring in physics-only G3

Dev-only feasibility study in Mikhail's lane. No submission/candidate change,
no camera dispatch and no sealed records. Model calls/tokens/spend: **0 / 0 / $0**.
**The registered panel passes:** language full successes **0/10 → 5/10**;
single-cup guard **6/10 → 10/10**. All five language successes retain all final
fluid predicates. Both official and full-success-only gates pass.

## Checker and language contract

Read public `task/RoboDojo/tasks/pour_by_language.py` and
`pour_liquid_into_cup.py` before any controller execution. Language allows
800 steps and names three ordered bottle/bowl pairs by color and ordinal.
There is no selectable liquid or requested amount. Public category indices map
to red/turquoise/violet bottles and black/white/brown bowls. The route uses the
instruction clauses and observed object colors, with a global assignment; it
never reads target checker labels. Internal handles become instance names.

Each bottle must cross into upright (axis within 30°) after its fluid predicate
passes. Language requires >=95% capture of **poured** liquid, <=10% residual,
zero box buffers and 1 mm target-bottom clearance. Disconnected outside
components smaller than 12 particles at 6 mm XY connectivity can be ignored,
up to 20% of the original particles. The denominator excludes residual and
ignored particles: raw capture alone is not the official predicate. Single-cup
allows 400 steps, >=97% capture, <=15% residual, 6.5 mm connectivity and component
size 7. Full checker/source hashes are in the result artifact.

Language banks ordered tiers 20/50/100 on home edges. Its nested home condition
ORs the arms: an idle arm staying home prevents another rising edge. We add no
idle-arm motion to manufacture credit. Report both official scores and a
full-success-only score (100 for full success, otherwise zero). A positive
feasibility result also requires a full success with all three final fluid
predicates true. G3 diagnostics run **after** the episode and never feed policy.
A pending upright check and a current fluid predicate are distinct: later motion
can scatter a bowl whose earlier check already latched.

## Implementation and unchanged defaults

`ported_pour_profile=off` is mirrored in RuntimeConfig, Skills and G3.
Opt-ins `sparse`/`via` select original v2; `checked` selects original v5 and its
ordered composite. Holding metadata propagates so checked held recovery cannot
fall through to generic open/finish. Off retains the previous finish behavior.
The existing default-off `pour_order` route now normalizes case and refuses
incomplete/repeated-color three-clause assignments. Valid public instructions
retain their original routing.

`ported_pour_pace` still defaults to **v1**. New opt-in **budget** retains overlap's
flow angles/speed, holds, drain12 and staged untilt angles. Lift uses normal
speed; untilt uses 1.0 within the existing held limit. Only the last flow
profile can spend the put-back reserve; its native move budget, leases and all
safety checks remain. Its completion flag requires 150°, profile end and <=30°
upright. Legacy completion semantics remain unchanged. Single-cup routing
ignores pace; its B change in this panel is aim only. No candidate or submission
configuration is changed.

## Interpretation and scope

The fresh panel establishes that language pouring works in perfect-state native
physics with this opt-in combination. Five full successes finish in **655–750**
steps. Across their fifteen fluids, minimum raw capture of poured particles is
**99.78%**, maximum residual is **4.69%**, and at most **4/1921** particles are
outside. All fifteen public fluid predicates are true at episode end.

The remaining failures have concrete grasp, slip, return-tracking and upright-
budget evidence; physics and the checker do not make the task impossible.
Aim-only and version/profile substitutions were insufficient in pilots. The
paired panel measures budget+b10 together; it cannot assign separate effect
sizes to those two changes. This is feasibility on ten fresh layouts, with wide
sample spread, not a camera result or an estimate of board Average gain.

The [parked camera A/B design](2026-11-07-pour-camera-ab-spec.md) isolates pacing
with aim b10 held equal, includes reference and strong guards plus prospective
write auditing, and requires a new registration/source/host assignment. No
camera job is queued. The current compute3 renderer remains prohibited.
