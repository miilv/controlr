# 2026-10-28 — Background (continuous) perception: does perception on every step's frames make the belief right at grasp / place time?

Stream C of the perception-to-95 % push (PERCEPTION_95.md). Journal: [../journal/2026-10-28-dojo-bgperc.md](../journal/2026-10-28-dojo-bgperc.md).
Code: branch `feat/dojo-bgperc` (`dojo/perception/bgperc.py`, `seq_capture.py`, `seq_data.py`, `dojo/runtime/bg.py`,
`scripts/perception/bgperc/`).

## Question

The runtime perceives at a few discrete points (frame 0, scan, looks, the pre-grasp refine) and ignores the frames in
between, although both wrist cameras sweep the table at every step. Does a background process that consumes those
frames, re-estimates the uncertain objects and the ones the next grasp / place needs, and fuses the evidence per
object, raise the share of clean events (stream M's grasp-time metric) over the runtime's own belief? What does it cost
per step? Is SAM 3.1's video tracker a better frame-to-frame source than re-running refine per frame?

## Data and protocol

- **Sequences (proxy tier: 5.1 physics-only + the 6.0 render, compute3).** 19 recorded runs, replayed action by action
  with `dojo.perception.seq_capture`. The replay is exact: it gives the same `dev_gt_probe` as the recorded run. All
  three cameras + state + dev GT are kept at every step, as JPEG q95 4:4:4 with frame 0 also as PNG. JPEG vs PNG:
  B1 8/8 objects the same, refine 14/14 the same within 2 mm.
- **Episodes (ids 100000-09 only):**
  - hang_mugs 100000-04 (P3r3, submit_v2);
  - organize_table 100000-04 (B_breadth2, submit_v1);
  - stack_bowls 100000-01 (AGB);
  - deposit_coin 100006 (S79) + 100001-03 (B_breadth2);
  - build_tower 100000 / 02 / 04 (P3r3, submit_v2).
  - 118 grasp + place events in all. 6,798 replayed observations (~19.6k images), 2.0 GB as JPEG.
- **Scoring: stream M's `dojo.perception.grasp_metric` (main, #204), unchanged.**
  - `episode_events(report, actions)` gives each grasp / place from the runtime's own logs: its target / reference,
    the approach corridor, and the runtime's belief.
  - `score_event` scores a belief against GT at step k - 1, taken from the sequence's own rows.
  - Place events carry `m95_score.held_drift` / `held_gap`.
  - On the episodes both of us scored, my `rt` arm matches M's tables exactly: hang_mugs 100000 4/6 clean,
    100003 1/4; stack_bowls 100000 1/6, 100001 0/2.
  - R = 10 cm unless stated.
- **Arms.** All arms share M's event attribution and the in-hand propagation of moved objects.
  - `rt`: the runtime's own belief, rebuilt by M's `BeliefTracker`: the planning record (frame 0 + scan + looks),
    then its refines, drops and post-place re-measures.
  - `f0`: `rt`, but every object the runtime has not moved yet sits at its frame-0 B1 pose (the run's own `initial`
    record). This is frame-0 perception only.
  - `bg_rt`: `rt` + background refines (`BgConfig` defaults):
    - every 2nd step, at most one wrist refine with GL silhouettes, on the highest-priority object in clear view;
    - priority: required by the next event > in doubt / suspect > unconfirmed / low confidence;
    - a fit within 6 mm confirms the object;
    - a fit elsewhere moves its position only when two fits >= 3 steps apart agree within 6 mm, or one fit has
      IoU >= 0.9 and visible >= 0.8 within 15 mm. The orientation is kept;
    - an empty place in clear view is absence evidence, and the object is searched (radius 6 cm) at its next view.
  - `bgc_rt`: `bg_rt` + contact suspects. A jaw inside an untouched object's footprint, below its top, makes the
    object searched at its next view.
  - Background evidence counts for an event only if it is >= 15 steps older than the event (the plan's descent was
    made before that). It also counts only for objects nobody touched or re-measured since: each object has an epoch,
    and a grasp, a release or any runtime re-measure starts a new one.
- GT is read by the scorer only; the background reads only the official frames and the belief. 0 LLM calls.

## Results

### Baseline: where the runtime's own belief fails (19 episodes, 118 events)

| task | episodes | events | rt clean R10 | f0 clean R10 | f0 fixed / broken vs rt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| build_tower | 3 | 54 | 54 | 51 | 0 / 3 |
| deposit_coin | 4 | 4 | 2 | 0 | 0 / 2 |
| hang_mugs | 5 | 24 | 14 | 13 | 1 / 2 |
| organize_table | 5 | 28 | 18 | 12 | 0 / 6 |
| stack_bowls | 2 | 8 | 1 | 1 | 0 / 0 |
| **all** | 19 | 118 | **89 (75 %)** | 77 (65 %) | 1 / 13 |

- R5 / R10 / R15: rt 92 / 89 / 89, f0 81 / 77 / 74.
- rt's R10 failure reasons (one event can have several): phantom 12, held not_held 11, neighbour not found 9,
  neighbour off 6, target gross 4, held overshoot 3, target overshoot 2, held rot 2, target identity / rot,
  reference gross / rot 1 each.
- The scan, looks and refines are worth +12 net events over frame-0 perception.
- What fails is mostly not "the next target":
  - phantoms and wrong neighbours: objects B1 put 80-200 mm off. The organize_table keyboard is one in 3 of 5
    episodes;
  - the held state;
  - placed objects that settled elsewhere, e.g. mugs swinging on the holder.

### Background perception vs the runtime's own belief (paired, same events)

| episodes (scorer) | events | rt clean R10 | arm | arm clean R10 | fixed / broken vs rt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| build_tower 3, deposit_coin 4, hang_mugs 100000 (#204) | 64 | 60 | refines (`bg_rt`) | 60 | 0 / 0 |
| same | 64 | 60 | refines + contact (`bgc_rt`) | 60 | 0 / 0 |
| play_tic_tac_toe 100000-02, 100004 (#205, stride 2) | 87 | 44 | refines + contact | 44 | 0 / 0 |
| same | 87 | 44 | + head checks + doubt re-reads (`bgd_rt`) | 46 | 3 / 1 |
| organize_table 100000 (#205) | 9 | 6 | + head checks + doubt re-reads | 6 | 0 / 0 |

- The other 10 hang / organize / bowls episodes were not re-run with `bgd_rt`: there it costs 1.2 s per step
  (re-reads 7.7 s each, refines 2.2 s), and the verdict did not depend on them.
- On organize_table 100000 the "doubt" request fired on 2/3 unclean vs 0/6 clean events.

- **What it does on these frames.** On build_tower / deposit_coin / hang_mugs 100000 the background confirms most
  untouched objects (rt was already right there). It makes 8-14 moves, and none of them changes an event's class:
  0 fixed / 0 broken.
- **Before the fix, it broke one event.** A move that took a fit's 7-degree turn made build_tower 100002's block5
  "rot" wrong. Since then moves are position-only, and any runtime re-measure starts a new epoch.
- **Why local refines cannot reach most failures:**
  - gross B1 errors are 80-200 mm off, beyond a refine's 6 cm search;
  - the held state belongs to stream H, released / placed poses to stream W;
  - knocked objects are found, but late. hang_mugs 100000's mug0 was knocked at step ~300; the background saw its
    place empty at 316 and found it 51 mm away at 354 / 358, after the re-grasp at 338.
- **Look requests.**
  - "Unconfirmed + low confidence" fires on clean events too often to spend a look on.
  - "Doubt" (absence seen twice) is rare on these 8 episodes: 0 of the 4 unclean events, 1 of the 60 clean ones. With
    head checks on tic-tac-toe it fires on 16/41 unclean vs 7/46 clean events.

### Tic-tac-toe (actor-moved objects; stride-2 sequences, grasp_metric #205)

- **The opponent never moved a piece in 100000-02.** Its pieces are at their frame-0 poses on every step (GT), so there
  is nothing for a watch read to add there. The 13 "neighbour opponent_piece not found" events in M's tables come from
  B_breadth 100001 and B_breadth2 100004. 100004 is rendered and scored below.
- **The ttt failures in these episodes are a dropped own piece.** player_piece0 was not held at its place (105 mm), and
  the next 6 events were wrong about it: target gross 105 mm, phantom 109-123 mm.
- **What the head check sees, and what it misses.**
  - On 100004 it sees the opponent's emptied start place: precision 0.0 on 11/11 checks once the place is visible.
    The pieces still at their places never read below 0.70: 0 of 675 checks over the 4 episodes
    (`~/dojo-data/bgperc/ttt_headcheck.json`).
  - It cannot see that a believed place ON the board is empty (100000's dropped player_piece0), because the board under
    it is foreground too.
- **B1 cannot re-find the moved piece.** Doubt-triggered re-reads (B1 on the head view, 0.26 s each on chessmen) ran
  13 times after the move. None paired a reading with the moved piece: B1's placement priors keep the opponent's
  pieces on their side of the table. So the background did not fix 100004's 5 "neighbour opponent_piece0 not found"
  events. The skill-level fix is PR #215 (the watch's cell, gated on the same head check).
- **Its 3 fixed / 1 broken come from own pieces.** Two agreeing fits once put player_piece4 on a look-alike chessman's
  spot (100000, broken). The fixed events are neighbour checks on the dropped player_piece0.

### Cost

- **Background refines.** 1.0-1.5 s per refine with GL on mug / chessman-scale meshes, at most 1 refine per 2 steps.
  That comes to 166 ms per replayed step with refines alone (8 episodes) and 199 ms with contact suspects. On
  tic-tac-toe at stride 2, where every observation is processed, it is 480-550 ms per observation.
- **Head checks.** ~0.2 s per check, every 10 steps.
- **B1 re-reads.** 0.26 s on the chessmen (head only), 19 s on hang_mugs (head only), 43 s with the wrists.
- **Where the cost lands.** In the runtime, `bg_step` runs in the perception service at each observation: it costs wall
  time, not env steps. One extra observation every 4 steps (`bg_obs_every`) adds ~0.12 s of proxy render plus
  0.3-0.5 s of background per 4 steps, about +30-60 % of a 400-step episode's ~250-580 s wall. That fits the step
  budget (0 steps). The power cost is one GL context (~0.3 GB) and one CPU core.

### SAM 3.1 video tracker (frame-to-frame masks)

- **Point-only prompts fail.** With Meta's sam3 at f1647e8 and the Comfy-Org multiplex fp16 checkpoint, a
  point-prompted object gets a mask on its prompt frame only. Later frames are empty in a 150-frame left-wrist
  sequence and a 180-frame head sequence (hang_mugs 100000; mIoU 0.01-0.05 against the GT visible masks).
- **Text first works, at a VRAM cost.**
  - The multiplex model merges its SAM2-style partial propagation into the detector's per-frame outputs. One text
    prompt plus a full detector pass first makes the clicked object propagate (20/20 frames).
  - That pass takes 18-21 GB of VRAM on 60 frames at 640x480, even with a text that matches nothing (OOM twice on the
    shared 32 GB card). With "object" as the text it detects everything.
- **Per-frame wrist refine, for comparison.** On hang_mugs 100000's left wrist, 65 calls from GT + 8 mm priors:
  0.63 s per call, ok 61/65; mIoU against the GT visible masks 0.93 (mug1, 26 frames), 0.65 (mug0, 34), 0.71
  (mug2, 5); median error 1.5 / 12.5 / 9.9 mm.
- **Verdict: DROP the SAM 3.1 tracker** for frame-to-frame tracking on our boxes. It is not usable without the
  detector pass, and with it it does not fit the shared GPU. The frame-to-frame source stays the per-frame refine
  (or SAM 2.1-S clicks, #196) when needed.

## Verdict

- **DROP background refines / re-reads as a belief writer, for now.**
  - 13 episodes / 160 events: 0 fixed / 0 broken without head checks (12 episodes, 151 events). With head checks:
    3 / 1 on tic-tac-toe and 0 / 0 on organize_table 100000.
  - The runtime's own pre-grasp refine already gets the targets right.
  - What remains is beyond local evidence:
    - gross B1 errors 80-200 mm off (the organize_table keyboard);
    - the held state (stream H, #209);
    - released / placed poses (stream W, #213);
    - objects an actor moved off their generator region. B1 re-reads cannot re-find these: its placement priors keep
      the piece on its start side (ttt 100004: 13 head re-reads after the move, none paired a reading with the moved
      piece).
- **KEEP, default off, as infrastructure** (PR, this branch):
  - the per-step sequences, which streams W, H and V already use tonight;
  - `BackgroundPerceiver` with epochs, absence, doubt and needs_look;
  - `RuntimeConfig.bg_perception`, `bg_step`, `Bridge.obs_every` / `on_obs`.

  This is where between-event signals fuse, once they come from a region-free source: V's `b1_doubt` (taken from
  frames where the gripper is not over the object; V measured that wrist views at the close are worse than frame 0),
  SAM 2.1 clicks, or S's detector.
- **ADOPT** (separate PR #215): a watch-confirmed opponent move moves that piece in the belief. Gated by the head view:
  0 of 675 false "empty" reads on 4 tic-tac-toe replays. It addresses 100004's 5 "neighbour not found" events, 13 in
  M's tic-tac-toe table.
- **Look trigger.** "Doubt" with head checks fires on 16/41 unclean vs 7/46 clean tic-tac-toe events. That is a
  candidate for targeted looks, but only once a look can relocate the object (same caveat as above).
- **SAM 3.1 tracker: DROP** (above).
- **Separate item for S:** general_pickup's 13 failures are clutter distractors near the corridor that B1 never
  estimates (M's table).

## Spend

0 LLM calls. GPU (all compute3, heavy1, one C job at a time):
- 19 + 15 per-step replay renders, ~2-4 min each;
- GL evaluation jobs, 3-8 min per episode;
- 3 SAM 3.1 jobs (two killed by the VRAM guard);
- one head-check emptiness test (ttt, 4 episodes).
CPU <= 4 threads per eval. Disk on compute3: 2-3.3 GB (frames).
