## Interpretation and limits

The wide hold can survive the lift, but carries still shift the headset by
roughly 90–110 mm. A successful partial score is not evidence of a reliable
hold. Existing put-down/re-grasp recovery participates in the scoring standard
row. `not_home` is G3's diagnostic category, not a demonstrated causal failure
of the home move: several such rows report `finish=ok` and partial score.
The authoritative outcome here is the task checker's final score.

The laptop 80-point tier remains blocked on picking the closed slab. No new
laptop pick is claimed: prior edge/overhang failures were not re-run, and lid
closure alone has no partial credit. Future work needs a stable headset hold
through reorientation and a genuinely different laptop support/grasp mechanism.

job was dispatched on the incompatible driver. Saved action and numeric trace
