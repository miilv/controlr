# CLASSIFY2: native classification diagnostic

## Prospective protocol

This is a mechanism diagnostic, not an adoption panel. Zero LLM calls or camera
jobs. The native route, carry and repair switches default off. Baseline is
`ported_routes=on, ported_classify=p2`; numbered labels are the perception
baseline. Physics has perfect identities and cannot measure camera retention.

Generate private layouts 100000–100009 under salt
`classify2-20261107-native-diagnostic-01`, with identical settled records in each
arm. Do not use any other ids or sealed records. First diagnose 100000–100002:
A = p2, B = native/top/central, C = B + Cartesian carry, D = C + budgeted repair.
These deliberately test several mechanisms; B combines grasp and placement and
cannot isolate their individual effects. Stop on infrastructure failure; missing
or invalid rows are not scores. A subsequent confirmatory panel requires a new
salt and at least ten pairs per cell, frozen candidates, score spread and stage
metrics. No adoption is claimed from these diagnostics.

Measure score, steps, pick planning failures, pick survival, placement status,
held-object aborts, repair actions and reserve refusals. Category assignment cost
is a geometric lower bound in metres, not predicted native actions. Compare
wrong-basket placements using final physics objects offline. Identity retention
and carry/tilt slips require trace instrumentation; unavailable metrics remain
unmeasured, never inferred from evaluator termination. The controller never
interprets continuing after home as failure.

The requested REPRO bar `data/rsi-yardstick/per_cell.json` is absent from this
checkout and compute3's main deployment at registration. Historical external
seed-0 95.2/84.4 and clean D-C19 34/8 are context only, not paired yardsticks.

Internal pick/lift verification accepted 9/9 executed picks in free E, 5/5
in F and G, 8/8 in language B, and 4/4 in language C and D. This is the skill's
own lift check, not an independent contact-trace survival measurement.

Final wrong-basket placements are zero under the same AABB containment test for
these native rows. That low count reflects limited placement coverage and does
not validate spill prevention. Final geometry comes from offline physics poses,
never from checker feedback in the controller. The receipt omits a baseline
wrong-basket comparison because p2's private naming differs from world labels.

Identity is perfect by construction in G3. Mask→proposal→world retention and
category confusion are **unmeasured**, not 100%. The known watch loss remains
open: the prior permitted-dev diagnosis found 1/12 watches retained, 8/30 member
slots occupied by basket shadows, and fragmented foreground on 7/11 missed
watches (journal 2026-10-29-dojo-bylang-watch-trace). Those are historical numbers,
not new measurements or evidence that this PR fixes retention.

The repair switch is bounded and tested, but has no physics efficacy evidence:
all revised rows abort holding before a repair round. The distance-only basket
objective has no native-action/relay cost calibration. Finger-line alignment
before tilt, car/pen-specific grasp/carry selection, a relay planner, safe
held-object recovery and persistent candidate/wrist-confirmation records are
**not implemented** here. The generic finish can release a held object after
abort; this is an explicit reason to keep the diagnostic draft and off, rather
than request camera evaluation of this route. No criterion reads continued
execution after home as failure.
